# What Destructibles.tla stands for

`models/Destructibles.tla` checks a stage's heft destructibles (W45-C): a piece of tier 1 to 5 that a ball of that heft class breaks by rolling into it, or that a blast breaks one class higher; the scrap and cogs a break drops against the stage's cap; the line, mount and aim that can rest on a piece; and the stage retry that rebuilds the pieces and restores the run. This page maps each step to the code. `scripts/gate/model-receipts.mjs` reads every source path on this page as the list of covered files, and the last line as the sync commit.

The covered files:

- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/destructibles.gd`: the pieces, the break, the payout and its meter.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/world.gd`: the world tick's `on_ball`, and the module built on each stage entry and freed with the stage.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/movement/player_ball.gd`: the ball's blasts and its `_on_piece_shattered` listener.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/movement/augments.gd`: the line's `attach`, the world aim, and the mount's face.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/game.gd`: `aim_along`, the one place `kit.aim` is set.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/core/context.gd`: `break_props`, the combat blast sources' door.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/core/flow.gd`: `enter_stage` and `retry_stage`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/core/run_state.gd`: `snapshot` and `restore`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/build/build.gd`: the pickups that fill the wallet, and the stage entry that lays the scrap field out again.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/build/scrap_field.gd`: `drop`, `drop_cogs`, and `lay_out`, which clears the field first.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/contraptions.gd`: `_break_lane_props`, a works surge's blast on the props in its lane (`on_blast` with tier bonus 1, the model's `OnBlastBonus`).

## Steps

| Model step                   | Code                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `WorldTick`, `OnBall(p)`     | world.gd `_physics_process` calls `on_ball(ball, velocity, ctx.run.heft_class(), delta)` once a physics tick. `on_ball` skips a piece that is not intact, and breaks one whose tier is at most `mini(TIER_MAX, heft_class + clampi(_swing_heft_bonus, 0, 1))` when the ball is fast enough (`_bar`); anything else is a wall. The model lets the ball meet any one piece a tick, so it does not read the sweep's geometry or the speed                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `BallTick`                   | the ball's own physics tick, once a frame, in either order with the world tick (`ran`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `LatchLine`                  | player_ball.gd `_press_harpoon`: `grapple_to(kit.aim.point, ...)`, with no new ray. A piece is in no body group, so its aim carries no body and its line is a world line (augments.gd `_world_aim`, `attach`: `on_body` false)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `MountPiece(p)`              | augments.gd `_mount`: the face `_mount_spot` finds, by ray (`_steepest_face`), or, with no face in reach, the anchor of a world line, with no ray                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `ReleaseBall`                | the harpoon released (`release_grapple`) or the mount left; the model drops both at once                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `AimPiece(p)`, `AimAway`     | game.gd `aim_along`: `kit.aim` is what the crosshair ray hits, set outside the ball's tick. A ray meets a piece only while its collider is live                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `OnBlast(p, b)`              | `on_blast(at, radius, source, tier_bonus, heft_class)`, called by the ball's slam and Redline (player_ball.gd `_break_props`), every combat source through context.gd `break_props` (the ultimates, sticky, silo battery, quake knock, det_cord, tether_slam), and contraptions/sluice washes. Every player blast passes `run.heft_class()` and clamps `tier_bonus` via `clampi(tier_bonus, 0, 1)`; the model also lets a source ask for 2, to check the clamp. Specialized doors `on_taut_swing` and `on_hurled_enemy` share the same `+1` ceiling                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `OnCapCharge(p)`             | `on_blast(..., Consumables.CAP_CHARGE, 0, Destructibles.TIER_MAX)` (W47-C1): intentional pocket breaching charge with `effective := TIER_MAX` (consumables_test.gd verifies Tier 5 shatter at Heft I paying strictly once); routes through `_near`, `not p.intact`, and `_shatter`, so it obeys `PaysOnce`, `NoLineOnRubble`, `NoColliderAfterShatter`, and `RetryNoFarm` without tripping `TierCapNeverStacks` |
| `ShatterCleanup(p)`          | `_shatter`, one call inside `on_ball`, `on_blast`, or any specialized break door: `collision_layer` 0 at once (the shape is disabled deferred), `_unfile`, `_pay`, then `piece_shattered`, whose one listener is player_ball.gd `_on_piece_shattered` (bound by `bind_destructibles`): it cuts a world line anchored on the piece, drops a mount whose face is on it, and clears an aim at it. The listener runs inside the emit, so nothing comes between the break and the release (VFM-06)                                                                                             |
| `ShatterCleanup`, the payout | `_pay`: `LOOT[tier]` (a tier 1 piece pays 1 scrap half the time), each clamped to what `scrap_cap - _scrap_paid` and `cog_cap - _cogs_paid` leave, dropped as pickups at the piece's foot (scrap_field.gd `drop`, `drop_cogs`); tier >= 3 pieces also roll up to `consumable_cap` (3 a stage, `_consumables_paid` zeroed in `teardown()`) consumable drops via `_combat.drop_consumable`. On a scrapless build `_pay` returns first and pays nothing (P6); the model's payout over-approximates it, since paying less is inside every rule, so it has no constant (VFM-04)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `Collect`                    | build.gd: `scrap.step` hands each pickup the ball reaches to `run.add_scrap`, and each gear to `run.add_cogs`; combat `_step_consumables` collects dropped consumables into `run.consumables`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `RetryStage`                 | flow.gd `retry_stage` (a held R in a storeless flow; the death screen's stage replay goes through it): `run.restore()` puts `scrap`, `cogs`, and `consumables` back to the stage-entry `snapshot` (`enter_stage` takes it before `stage_entered`), then `stage_entered(stage, true)`. Its handlers rebuild the pieces (world.gd `_host_destructibles` frees the old module; `build` calls `teardown`, which zeroes `_scrap_paid`, `_cogs_paid`, and `_consumables_paid`), lay the scrap field out again (build.gd `scrap.lay_out`, which calls `clear()` first, so every uncollected drop goes), and clear dropped consumables (`_clear_consumables_runtime`) |
| `StageExit`, `Death`         | the launch to the next stage, and a death; nothing after them is modelled except a retry from the death screen                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `FrameEnd`                   | the end of a physics frame, where `NoLineOnRubble` is read (VFM-08)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

The wallet counts from the stage-entry checkpoint, so the checkpoint itself is 0 in the model.

## Guards

| Constant                              | The guard in the code                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Config that turns it off                        | Rule that must break     |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `BlastNeedsIntact`, `UnfileOnShatter` | `on_blast`'s `not p.intact` skip, and `_unfile`, which takes a shattered piece out of the grid `_near` reads. Either alone keeps a blast off rubble: `destructibles-belt-repay` turns the first off and reports no break (lesson VFM-11) | `destructibles-broken-repay` (both off)         | `PaysOnce`               |
| `CapClamp`                            | `_pay`'s `clampi(mini(n, cap - paid), 0, n)`                                                                                                                                                                                             | `destructibles-broken-nocap`                    | `PaysOnce`               |
| `CutLine`                             | `_on_piece_shattered`: `cut_line("shattered")`                                                                                                                                                                                           | `destructibles-broken-cutline`                  | `NoLineOnRubble`         |
| `DropMount`                           | `_on_piece_shattered`: `kit.mounted = false`                                                                                                                                                                                             | `destructibles-broken-dismount`                 | `NoLineOnRubble`         |
| `ClearAim`                            | `_on_piece_shattered`: `kit.aim = Augments.no_aim()`                                                                                                                                                                                     | `destructibles-broken-staleaim`                 | `NoLineOnRubble`         |
| `ColliderOff`                         | `_shatter`: `p.body.collision_layer = 0`                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `destructibles-broken-collider`                 | `NoColliderAfterShatter` |
| `RetryRestoresRun`                    | `retry_stage`: `run.restore()`                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `destructibles-broken-retryfarm`                | `RetryNoFarm`            |
| `RetryClearsDrops`                    | the retry's `scrap.lay_out` -> `clear()`                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `destructibles-broken-keepdrops`                | `RetryNoFarm`            |
| `BonusCap`                            | `on_blast`: `clampi(tier_bonus, 0, 1)`                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `destructibles-broken-tierstack` (`BonusCap` 2) | `TierCapNeverStacks`     |

`on_ball`'s own `not p.intact` skip has no constant: `_unfile` backs it the same way, and no caller reaches a piece any other way.

## Bounds

Two pieces, of tiers 1 and 3, every heft class from 1 to 3, two retries. The caps are 3 scrap, 1 cog, and 1 consumable (`ConsCap = 1`), so that two pieces reach them; the real caps are 35, 20, and 3 (`CONSUMABLE_CAP`) over the pieces the dam and the flume place: 14 each at the sync commit, 15 and 14 on stage/alpha-w45 since P3 (38332087c), still inside the 12 to 15 that `sketches/core-loop/tests/destructibles_world1_test.gd` holds a world 1 stage to. Neither count changes the model's bounds. A tier 3 piece pays 3 scrap, 2 cogs, and optionally 1 consumable, so the cog and consumable caps bind on one break and the scrap cap on two. With tiers 1 and 3, a class 1 ball's blast stops at tier 2 and a stacked bonus would reach tier 3; a class 2 blast breaks tier 3 through its +1, and `OnCapCharge` breaks tier 3 at any heft class while obeying `PaysOnce`.

Run `bash models/make-destructibles-configs.sh`, then plain TLC on each `destructibles-*.cfg` with `Destructibles.tla`.

| Config                     | Stands for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Must report                                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `destructibles-today`      | the code at the sync commit, every guard in place                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | no break, in 83,322 distinct states (>= pinned 27,096)              |
| `destructibles-belt-repay` | `on_blast` without its intact check, `_unfile` still in place                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | no break (the second guard holds)                                   |
| `destructibles-broken-*`   | one guard off, as the Guards table lists                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | the named rule breaks                                               |
| `destructibles-reach-*`    | one probe each that says "never" about a thing that must happen: each action fires (`OnBall`, `OnBlast`, `OnBlastBonus`, `CapCharge`, `Aim`, `Latch`, `Mount`, `Release`, `Collect`, `Retry`, `Exit`, `Death`), the listener frees each of a line, a mount and an aim, each cap fills (`ScrapCap`, `CogCap`, `ConsCap`), and a second attempt banks scrap and cogs | the probe breaks; one that holds means an action can no longer fire |

`node scripts/gate/model-receipts.mjs --out <dir> --row destructibles-safe` writes the receipt from `destructibles-today` and the nine `destructibles-broken-*` plants.

## The tests behind it

`sketches/core-loop/tests/destructibles_hooks_test.gd` plays the listener in the running module: the aimed piece shattered before the press, a line cut by a far shatter, a line on another piece left standing, a mount dropped, and a retry that rebuilds the module (its T9 and H4 rows). destructibles_test.gd covers the tier rule, the speed bar and the payout; consumables_test.gd covers `CAP_CHARGE` Tier 5 single-payout and retry belt restore; w47_h2_heroes_test.gd covers `on_hurled_enemy` and `on_rail_breach`. The model checks the orders those tests do not write down.

## Not modelled

The sweep's geometry and the speed bar (a piece the ball meets either breaks or is a wall), the refused cue and its chips, shrapnel and the debris strike (assumed not to trigger a synchronous nested `on_blast` inside `_shatter` before `piece_shattered.emit`, since `_on_piece_shattered` reads `last_shattered_id()`; no `enemy_struck`/`enemy_killed` listener does so today), fixed-cap specialized break doors (`on_segment_cut` clamped to tier 2, `on_rail_breach`/`on_piton_breach` clamped to tier 3) and enemy-mass blasts (drill_crew.gd `drill_core_jet`, plated_brute.gd `brute_charge`) which all route through `_near` + `not p.intact` + `_shatter`, the shards and puffs, more than one piece broken in one call (each break is a step of its own, which over-approximates), a heft class that changes mid-stage (each behaviour keeps one class), a line on a body (an enemy, a stake or a moving world anchor: `_on_piece_shattered` never cuts one, and a piece is none of them), the outpost mount (`mount_outpost`), the arena (it hosts no pieces), a Continue after a quit (`resume_run` loads the stage-entry save and calls `enter_stage` again, which rebuilds the pieces), lifetime tallies a pickup may feed outside the run, and anything drawn or heard.
