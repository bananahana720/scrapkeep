# What YardLock.tla stands for

`models/YardLock.tla` checks the Spillway's yard lock (the ring wall that shuts round the ball near the beacon and lands its crew in waves) and the overtime bell a won yard offers, under every interruption the run flow allows: a Warden that wakes (by the ring or a strike), breaks or vanishes, a death, a retry, a new run, a quit and a Continue, and the next stage's entry. This page maps each step to the code. `scripts/gate/model-receipts.mjs` reads every source path on this page as the list of covered files, and the last line as the sync commit.

The covered files:

- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/stage_locks.gd`: the lock's states, `_step_lock`, `_lock`, `_unlock`, `_on_flow_changed`, `_on_stage_entered` and `_clear`, and `_on_warden_engaged`, which sets `_engaged_s` and opens a shut yard in the same call (P7).
- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/lock_rows.gd`: the dam's and the flume's lock rows (`within_m`, `radius_m`, `ahead_m`, `max_s`, `waves`, `overtime`).
- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/overtime.gd`: `offer`, `ring`, `cash`, `clear`, `_step_touches`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/core/flow.gd`: `physics_step` (the ring entry and `_engage`), `show_start_card`, `start_pick_waits`, `enter_stage`, `retry_stage`, `_begin_run`, `resume_run`, `show_title`, `_on_player_died`, `_end_run`, `_unseal`, `_warden_vanished`, `_on_reward_chosen`, `_on_launched`, `_finish_stage`, `_set_state`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/core/beacon.gd`: `in_ring` (flat distance) and `advance`, which marks the ring entered.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/game.gd`: `_physics_process` calls `flow.physics_step`; the world module's `setup` adds StageLocks to the game root.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/world/world.gd`: `locks = StageLocks.new()` and `ctx.root.add_child(locks)`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/movement/player_ball.gd`: the ball's own tick, `reset_to`, and `state.grounded`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/movement/rigid_ball.gd`: a `RigidBody3D` with `custom_integrator` off, so the physics server moves it after every node's `_physics_process`; `_integrate_forces` sets `grounded`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/combat/combat.gd`: every hit emits `enemy_struck`, which wakes a sealed Warden.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/combat/weapons.gd`: `BASE` `range_m`, 85 m for the rapid laser, 70 for the arc caster and 95 for the pylon turret; combat.gd `_fire` multiplies it by `tuning.mod("weapon_range")`.
- `sketches/core-loop/src/build/parts.gd`: the range parts (`horizon_optic` 1.5, `long_barrel` and `focus_lens` 1.12 and 1.25), which make a shot reach at least 142.5 m (they multiply, so a build holding more than one reaches further).

The ball's place is one of four zones measured flat from the beacon. On the dam (beacon ring 30 m, `within_m` 200, so any ground counts inside 150 m; the yard centred `ahead_m` 10 m on from the ball, `radius_m` 26, `WALL_SLACK` 4, so a yard shut on a ball d metres out holds it as close as d minus 40): "ring" is inside the 30 m ring; "near" is outside it but within about 70 m, where a yard shut on the ball holds part of the ring (30 + 10 + 26 + 4 = 70 m); "circle" is the rest of the any-ground share, from about 70 m to 150 m; "out" is everything else, the incline's 150 to 200 m band included (it shuts the yard only on level ground, which the model counts as "circle").

## Assumptions

- `LongFlight`: the ball can reach the near or ring zone with the yard still armed, which needs it to cross the any-ground share (150 to about 70 m from the beacon) without touching ground: a jump, a launch, a harpoon swing or a fall from height. With it off, a ball that touches ground in the circle shuts the yard there, too far out to enter the ring from inside it. Every stage entry, retry and Continue places the ball outside the circle (the dam's entry is about 1,270 m out), so a rolling ball always meets the share first.
- `StrikeFrom`: the zones a ball can land a hit on the Warden from. The code's value is circle, near and ring: a yard shut at the share's 150 m edge holds the ball as close as 110 m, and with a horizon optic a shot reaches at least 142.5 m (the replay measured 127.2 m to the Warden from there; a build that also holds a long barrel or a focus lens multiplies further). A bare pylon turret (95 m) reaches from a yard shut nearer. From "out" the model lets no shot reach the Warden: with the 150 m share and at least 142.5 m of reach that is a modelling bound, and since P7 a hit from anywhere opens the yard in the same call, so a wider `StrikeFrom` adds only states. `yardlock-today-flight` narrows it to the ring.
- One locks tick runs out a wave's whistle; `max_s` is 5 locks ticks and the lock row 2 waves, so a won yard keeps time on its clock (as a 60 s lock does) and a slow one times out.

## Bounds

A run of two stages, both with a lock row (the dam and the flume), three stage entries a behaviour (retries, new runs and Continues count), two waves, `max_s` of 5 ticks, `MAX_RINGS` 3. The yard's panel and the start-card calls are counted per activation (per stage entry), capped at 2 so a plant that unlocks every tick stays finite.

## Configs

Run `bash models/make-yardlock-configs.sh`, then plain TLC on each `yardlock-*.cfg` with `YardLock.tla` (`LockAlwaysOpens` is a temporal property, so each config with it checks liveness under weak fairness of the three ticks and the end of the events between frames).

| Config | Stands for | Must report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `yardlock-today`, `yardlock-guarded` | the code at the sync commit, in a played game | no break, in at least the pinned 146,341 distinct states |
| `yardlock-today-nostore`, `yardlock-guarded-nostore` | the code in a storeless flow (tests, bots), every rule | no break, 133,885 distinct states |
| `yardlock-today-flight` | YL-1's near path on its own (`StrikeFrom` the ring) | no break |
| `yardlock-today-ranged` | YL-1's strike path on its own (`LongFlight` off) | no break |
| `yardlock-broken-*` | one guard off from `today`, as the Guards table lists | the named rule breaks |
| `yardlock-reach-*` | one probe each that says "never" about a thing that must happen: a lock shuts, shuts near the beacon, is won, times out, is skipped for an awake Warden, is skipped on an unsealed stage, lands a second wave, is spent by a flow change, the bell rings and is cashed, the Warden wakes, a strike lands on it, the waking opens a shut yard (by the ring and by a strike), the Warden breaks, the Warden vanishes, a Continue, a retry (storeless), the dead screen spends a shut yard (storeless), a lock on the second stage, and the "locked" guard on its own (`reach-Yield`, handler off) | the probe breaks; one that holds means an action can no longer fire |

`node scripts/gate/model-receipts.mjs --out <dir> --row yard-lock-safe` writes the receipt from `yardlock-today` and `yardlock-today-nostore` and the eleven `yardlock-broken-*` plants, the YL-1 and YL-2 regressions among them. P7 closed both findings, so neither is a gap.

## Not modelled

The Warden's clamp (`gate`, packs, `also`, its own `max_s`), the sentry, the wall's spring and bounce (a held ball stays in the zones the ring covers), the wave marks and sounds, more than one crew member, the bell's sluice and flood, the boss arena and victory lap (no lock row reaches them), seconds, and anything drawn or heard. `show_start_card`'s other callers (`_begin_run`, `retry_stage`, `resume_run`, `_engage`, the start card's own buttons, the workshop and the launch take) are outside the rule `StartCardOnce`, which counts the lock's two calls.
