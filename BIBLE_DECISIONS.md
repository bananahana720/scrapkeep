# Scrapkeep Bible: decisions for review

**Open choices accompanying the approved product reference · 6 September 2026.**

This is the historical BD source list. [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md#open-questions) defines the current questions and maps every BD identifier; [TRACKING.md](TRACKING.md#question-state-index) alone records answers and resolution state. Preserve these original alternatives when a choice is recorded there.

Andrew approved BIBLE.md as the source of truth for what the game is. This list retains choices explicitly left open in that document. The approval does not supply missing A/B/C picks, budgets or implementation rules.

The steam-cyberpunk junkyard direction, electromagnetic collection and fabrication, Southwest inspiration and the exact Section 7 style block are approved. The established four-world run, four slot trees, milestone progression and revival structure are also settled. Andrew also settled recursive binary branching during this review: one root → two → four → eight endpoints. They are not on this decision list.

## Sound and presentation proposal

BD-17 proposes dry physical instrumentation, workshop percussion and restrained synthesis, with regional arrangements of shared musical material. Threats and meaningful player actions lead the mix; earned build escalation earns more musical weight. Review [Audio & Feel](BIBLE.md#8-audio--feel) for that concrete direction. Timing, recorded assets, final mix and camera response remain untested production work.

## Image outliers and cohesion choices

The review found four image errors: Tarn's missing B label, the exposed mountain pilot, the combat HUD's revival/companion presentation, and the dashboard's six endpoints. Each received one automatic correction. Andrew then explicitly requested recursive binary branching, recorded as a separate U02 revision. This settles the tree's topology; it is no longer a decision gap.

Final image verdicts and any persistent outliers are recorded in the [cohesion report](COHESION_REPORT.md). Concept limitations remain around mounted equipment dimensions, rolling articulation in still art, the overview map's exaggerated white basin relief and final model detail. Those require later asset development, not a change to the approved pillars.

The manuscript review found no other material contradiction between the nine sections. The choices above concern unselected content and source gaps; none authorizes implementation. Production-engine selection and final hardware clearance also remain outside this document's approval scope.
