### Using Vertex Groups > Vertex Groups for Bones

Source: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/sculpt_paint/weight_paint/usage.html

Vertex groups are a primary tool for weight painting, especially around joints. You can manually adjust weights or create them from scratch. The process involves selecting the armature in Pose Mode, then the mesh in Weight Paint Mode, and finally selecting a bone to activate its vertex group and paint its weights. Blender automatically creates a vertex group if one doesn't exist when you start painting. For symmetrical models, the Mirror Vertex Groups option can automate weight creation for the opposite side.

When using 'With Empty Groups' for Armature Deform Parenting, empty vertex groups are created on child objects, named after each deforming bone in the armature.
