### SkeletonModifier3D > Method Descriptions > _process_modification_with_delta

Source: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_skeletonmodifier3d.html

Custom skeleton modifier logic can be implemented by overriding _process_modification_with_delta(), where current bone poses are retrieved and updated. This method is supplied with delta time from the parent Skeleton3D and may be executed with a delta of 0.0 outside standard node processing cycles to process modifications immediately upon skeleton initialization.

Source: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_skeletonmodifier3d.html

When implementing custom skeleton modifications, the modifier should always apply 100% of the calculated pose without manual interpolation or influence scaling. The parent Skeleton3D automatically blends and applies the modifier's influence value to all affected bone poses.

Source: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_boneattachment3d.html

When override_pose is set to true, the BoneAttachment3D node can change the pose of the bone it is attached to rather than following the bone's transform. Because this override operates interruptively during skeleton updates via signals, it may cause unintended behavior when used alongside SkeletonModifier3D.
