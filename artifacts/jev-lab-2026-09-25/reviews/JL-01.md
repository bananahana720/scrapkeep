### 3. Correctness risks

Non-blocking, in priority order:

a. `record.mjs:351` `seq = existing.split("\n").length - 1` assumes a newline-terminated, uncorrupted index. If a prior process died between `writeFile(run.json)` (`:361`) and the append (`:365`), or someone hand-edits the index without a trailing newline, the next run gets a duplicate seq and the append glues onto the last line.
