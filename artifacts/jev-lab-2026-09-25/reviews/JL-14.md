The record is still honest about what produced them (`run.json provenance.jev.model` = resolved, `provenance.instrument.model` = alias; replay run.json:34,39). A batch that mixes a hit and a live call is not silent: `scripts/lab/record.mjs:358-361` throws on the model change, `harness.mjs:318-334` catches it as cause `arm_throw`, effect `fail` (exit 1). - Why not blocking for this card: DoD 1 says "caches each verdict under `verdictKey()`, records the resolved model" (BACKLOG.md:5629), both met literally; plan § 2's key lists `model` as an instrument field, and the instrument's `model` was fixed as the alias by JL-03 (done). Re-keying is an instrument change outside this lease (`decide.mjs` "unchanged in shape", BACKLOG.md:5625). For replay specifically, serving the recorded verdicts is the intended semantics: a replay reproduces the recorded run, and the record names the model that produced it.
- Minimal safe behaviour now (two lines in `harness.mjs` around :325, could be a JL-14 revision or the next harness owner): before `run.jev(choice.jev)`, if `run.provenance.jev.model !== null && choice.jev.model !== run.provenance.jev.model`, abort with a new cause (`model_mismatch`) mapped to `invalid` in `ABORT_EFFECT` (`harness.mjs:87-94`), so a mixed batch exits 4, not 1. Carry to JL-18 (wires preflight into the harness): pass the preflight's resolved model into the arm and treat a hit whose `verdict.model` differs as a miss in a live batch and as `instrument_changed` in a replay. Longer term, pin the resolved model into the epoch record so the key moves with it.
