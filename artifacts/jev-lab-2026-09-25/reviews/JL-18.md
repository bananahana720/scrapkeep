## 6. Interpretation

Data across both sets (P(take_cache), cache listed first → last, same state and text within each pair): c1/q1 0.45→0.48; c2/q2 free-cache 0.38→0.52; swap-free current 0.32→0.49; swap-free neutral 0.15→0.50; costly swap current 0.24→0.38; neutral 0.10→0.19. push_objective sits in the middle position in both orders and is the option that gives up the mass (0.55→0.43, 0.59→0.45, 0.72→0.46, 0.86→0.76). Same-position repeat noise is 0.03-0.06 (free-cache vs swap-free current). lethal-option shows no position effect (push 0.78/0.76 whether last or first).

Weighing: (a) residual c2-specific text is the least supported, because c1's quite different option text (explicit buff list, terse push, held 0) gave the same reading at the same position; if framing is residual it lives in what c1 and c2 share (state `objective: reach_exit`, a middle push option that names the goal, three options with a second loot-like option), not in c2's edits. (b) position bias is real in these numbers: six of six pairs move the same way, 0.09-0.35 against 0.03-0.06 noise, largest under the neutral wording where the prior is weakest; it behaves like a last-position boost that only shows when the option is contested. (c) is the residual and the strongest: averaging over both orders gives ~0.45 under q2 and ~0.47 under q1, below 0.6 under either instrument, so removing position does not rescue free-cache. Most likely: (c) with (b) on top, i.e. Jev holds a prior of roughly 0.5 for the objective-directed option in a three-way choice that a free permanent buff does not overturn, and position tips the remainder. This is inference from 14 readings.

Pre-registered design to separate them, none of which changes the 0.6 line or the every-order rule: (1) run each threshold control in all six permutations, three repeats each, and report per-position P(take) with its spread; the pass rule stays "every order", and mean/min are reported beside it, so the position effect is measured rather than averaged away. (2) Comprehension nouls per option, asked in a separate call with no Choice: "Does this action cost any scrap?", "Does it take any time?", "Does the buff last the whole run?", "Does choosing it forgo the exit?" A wrong answer localises the failure to reading the option (a); right answers with the same Choice localise it to preference (c). (3) A third free-cache state whose push option does not name the exit or the charge ("Roll on along the current line; the cache stays unopened") and whose state omits `objective`, plus a two-option variant (take vs push) that removes the loot-splitting sibling and leaves only two orders. (4) A dominance probe outside caches, e.g. a free full heal at 8 HP with no threats: taken at ≥0.6 means the discount is specific to random buffs; not taken means Jev does not reliably take free dominant options in this domain. (5) Intent-only calls for one cell to test N7. Record all of it in c3's note before the first call, and report every cell as it falls.
