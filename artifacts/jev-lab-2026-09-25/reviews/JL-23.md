### Blocking

**B1. The first_red_s benefit is simulated for a runner that `watch` is not.**
- `testEntry` reports results only after the whole pool finishes. Line `scripts/play.mjs:279` is `for (const result of await inPool(jobs, limit))`, and `inPool` (`play.mjs:206-219`) returns only after `Promise.all` over every job. `runHeadless` (`play.mjs:180-204`) prints nothing while it runs.
- So in `watch`, every PASS/FAIL line and the RED verdict (`play.mjs:641-647`) appear only after the whole sketch run, whatever the order. The only user-visible effect of the ordering is the order of the printed lines.
- `simulatedFirstFail` (`scripts/lab/test-order.mjs:338-350`) times the moment the first failing check finishes. Its comment ("the first failing check's result… the way play.mjs's pool runs them") is true of execution order but false of when the result is seen.
- Two things compound this:
  - At default width, `jobLimit` gives up to 8 jobs (`play.mjs:297-305`) and ball-lab has 8 checks (`live/probe-watch.json:2-11`). With 10 or more cores, all 8 checks start together, so the order does not even change execution.
  - Neither limit is stated anywhere a user or the board would see it. A grep of `scripts/` finds no such note, and `watch` prints only "order: jev, forward/reverse gap …" (`play.mjs:650-654`).
- **Requirements violated:**
  - `ORCHESTRATOR.md:108`: every result must state its method and limits.
  - DoD 2 (`BACKLOG.md:5947`): first_red_s is measured, but on a model that does not match the delivered `watch`.
  - The plan's promised benefit (`plan.md:209`, "First red in about 8 s instead of a whole sketch run") is not delivered, and the receipts present 11.44 vs 25.15 s as if it were.
- **Fix, either one:**
  - (a) Re-state the result as "ranking quality, plus simulated time for a runner that streams results; currently unobservable in `watch`", and state both limits in `test-order.mjs` and the receipt. That alone would make this PASS+NOTES.
