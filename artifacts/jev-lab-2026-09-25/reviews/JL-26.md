- Inference: the instrument probably contributes to that lethality. Knocked names aren't exported, so I could not confirm which foes were knocked.
- This complies with the carried JL-21b rule, but any lethality or miniboss finding must be labelled as confounded. JL-28 will get zero power from 4 of the 6 Cable Bench cells. The coordinator should decide before JL-28 whether multi-hit foes need a different hiding rule; that would be a new instrument epoch.
