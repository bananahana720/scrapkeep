A Before you play

  1. Quiet the PC. Run the game fullscreen on DP-1 (VRR on). Close Nautilus, minimize the Claude app, and leave nothing else running on the 4080. Frame feel is part of the verdict.
  2. Set your old save aside. The sitting plays on a fresh profile that saves, so you feel the first-hour ladder across runs: Rivet freed, Tarn signing on, and the shift board opening items. The save lives in the game's data folder, the directory where Godot keeps a game's files under your home folder.

    This only renames the save; it deletes nothing. Your volume and display settings live in a separate file and stay as they are. To go back, rename the file back.

    cd ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/core-loop
mv profile.json profile.pre-w19-sitting.json
[ -f run.json ] && mv run.json run.pre-w19-sitting.json
    The last line also sets aside a half-played run, so the game can't resume it.
  3. Launch from the app menu. Press Super + Space to open the Omarchy launcher, type Scrapkeep, and pick the plain Scrapkeep entry. Don't pick "Scrapkeep sitting (all pilots, save untouched)": that one opens every pilot and doesn't save, so the ladder never moves. A fresh profile starts with Sable.

    If the game doesn't start, the AppImage probably can't mount itself. Install FUSE (the library that lets an AppImage mount itself), then launch again.

    sudo pacman -S fuse2
  4. Play two runs and time them. Make one run a straight line to Brindle, and one a completionist run that takes every cache, activity, secret and rogue. Note the wall-clock minutes of each. The bots take 8.2 min for the skipper run (489 bot-s) and 20.8 min for the completionist run (1249 bot-s). Your times against theirs set the final pacing target (ruling ag).

Record verdicts on the Sitting Kit. It saves as you tap, and the crew reads it from there.

World 1 passes when all 11 areas are marked and none is "Not yet". There must also be no open blocker bug, frames in budget, and zero placeholders.

01

Core feel

Rolling, ramming, the harpoon and the slam feel instant and joyful on the dam and the flume.

02

Wardens and bosses

The dam and flume Wardens, the rogue mini-boss in each park, and Brindle's three phases are readable, beatable and fair.

03

In-run growth

Grey panels on the ball, FIELD KIT and FLEET GRADE (cobalt) drops at Wardens and shop slot 1. A grown run feels mightier.

04

Progression across runs

The shift board, Rivet freed, Tarn on Brindle, the codex count at camp, and the timed door (Brindle under 12 min) give you a reason to go again.

05

Pilots

Sable first, then whoever unlocks. Each plays differently and has a character. The Cannon says its line.

06

Stage and world design

The dam, the flume and the Slag Bowl each test a skill, have lines to ride, and hide secrets and rib caches worth finding.

07

Look

Soft toy bodies, crisp machines, dawn to noon light, spray, mist and foam. The Slag Bowl crowd now has smooth-shell crew.

08

Sound and music

The Spillway's own event sounds and stings, Brindle's phase anthems, and the radio takes in the caches.

09

Clarity

Each mechanic is taught before you need it. The plate names the yard lock and the shut clamp. Warnings read in a crowd.

10

Difficulty and pacing

Runs last 10 to 15 min, with about 30 min for a completionist. Chaos you can learn. A strong build steamrolls. HARD opens after a win.

11

Stability

The game opens from the app menu, never crashes or blocks you, and holds its frames.
