# Tracking rule exercise

6 September 2026. Paper scenarios applied to the actual ORCHESTRATOR.md and TRACKING.md candidate. These are hypothetical states, not task execution, actual leases or completed sprint work. The independent reviewer separately checked these rules.

| Scenario | Hypothetical request | Required outcome | Governing rule and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate assignment | HF-01 already has worker A, attempt 1 and lease L1; worker B requests the same card. | Refuse B. Do not create another active lease or advance a dependency. | ORCHESTRATOR Admission requires ready state and conflict-free ownership; TRACKING ready → in_progress is the only initial assignment transition. Repeated handoff requests for the same accepted attempt attach to its existing result, not duplicate credit. |
| Overlapping files | A owns a section directory; B requests one file beneath it under another task. | Refuse or serialize B, even if task IDs or worktrees differ. | ORCHESTRATOR Admission explicitly treats directory descendants and generated/test/shared files as overlapping writes. Record a revised disjoint scope before assignment, never after an unauthorized edit. |
| Interrupted worker | A misses a checkpoint and appears disconnected; B requests its files. | Retain L1 until A/process is confirmed stopped, files and evidence inspected, and recovery recorded. | ORCHESTRATOR Ownership and interruption forbids time-based lease release. Late A output cannot overwrite B's later admitted attempt. |
| Failed independent review | A hands off a candidate that fails a DoD criterion. | Record changes_requested with actionable findings; issue attempt 2, then at most attempt 3. Persistent failure returns to blocked. | ORCHESTRATOR Handoff and review; TRACKING revised assignment requires readiness and lease checks. Attempt history and failed evidence remain. |
| Failed integration | Reviewer approves a candidate, but the coordinator's integrated checks fail. | Do not mark done or unlock any dependent; preserve failure evidence and isolate only safe coordinator changes. | ORCHESTRATOR Serial integration; TRACKING review → done requires checks on the exact integrated result. Candidate approval alone is insufficient. |
| Unlogged handoff | A posts “implemented” and a screenshot without candidate identity, complete packet or a board event. | Refuse accepted review/completion and downstream readiness. Request the complete packet, verify it, then log the transition. | TRACKING Event discipline and ORCHESTRATOR Handoff require recorded identity, actual checks, paths, failures and evidence limits. Chat activity grants no ownership or completion credit. |

Result: all six cases have a deterministic authority boundary and a non-completion outcome until their missing condition is satisfied. This is document-rule verification, not execution of a production coordinator or a runtime test.
