# Scrapkeep conflict and combat design swarm

Started 5 September 2026. This is a discovery and design pass. It produces a reviewable conflict, enemy and boss concept brief with explicit player counterplay, not gameplay implementation or a session goal.

## Phases

- [x] Frame: cover fiction/factions, enemy and boss encounters, and defensive combat in three independent research roles.
- [x] Fan out: collect three Astra research/design reports while the coordinator develops the shared conflict and interviews the publisher.
- [x] Aggregate: reconcile common combat rules, class fairness, roll/collection identity, and escalating scale.
- [x] Report: present original concepts, supporting references, unresolved choices, and a bounded next experiment.

Confirmed direction: Katamari-like rolling, collecting and bumping are central; cute animal pilots inside accumulated scrap; solo first, co-op later; 30–45-minute themed expeditions; combat-time autonomous infrastructure; companions assembled from loot; low-cooldown abilities; class-specific ultimate charging; unlock-only progression between runs. Physical growth currently stays within mech-scale routes. The extent of a colossal finale remains open.

All designs and suggested tuning are hypotheses until tested. No paid providers, installs, runtime changes, or production content commitments belong to this pass.
