## Prompt

Use-case: stylized-concept.
Create one wide landscape 16:9 original game concept image for SCRAPKEEP. It is an exploratory third-person gameplay art target, not an actual game screenshot. Use the attached image ONLY as a reference for the existing world's warm cream masonry, dark petrol scrap ball, safety-orange scavenged weaponry and mint energy; radically change the camera, scale and environment to match the following.

Camera and composition: immersive perspective close third-person chase camera just above and behind-left of a rolling spherical scrap mech, as if over its shoulder. Horizon and vast architecture visible. The entire sphere is visible in the lower-left foreground, occupying about one quarter of image height; its outline must stay readable. Camera is steady, low enough to feel the machine's weight, high enough to clearly see paths and enemies ahead. NOT an overhead isometric diorama.

Hero machine: a round dark petrol mechanical core with an upright gyroscopic glass capsule INSIDE its rolling cage. Through a clear side portion we can see one small original adorable orange foxlike animal pilot wearing a practical little flight harness, facing into the action; the animal is safely seated inside, not riding outside. Large rolling armor hoops move around the protected capsule. Asymmetrically attached scavenged orange cannons, cream armor scraps, mint battery coils and a few household-scale salvaged bits tell the story of a machine growing by collecting the world. Functional chunky mechanical silhouettes; not a humanoid mech, no legs, no giant human arms. One bold grappling cable from the ball launcher arcs taut to a rust-orange overhead crane anchor ahead, pulling the rolling ball around a banked corner. Convey weight with compressed scrap, kicked pebbles, and short dust streaks, not blur.

World: a cavernous abandoned foundry turned mechanical dungeon, with enormous vaulted industrial arches, overhead cranes and suspended workshops, warm cream stone mixed with copper plumbing and ancient machinery. Several connected readable combat spaces in depth, ramps and a high balcony, a clearly visible large gate leading deeper. Friendly handcrafted stylized shapes, strong light and shadow masses, a sense of an epic explorable place beyond the demo yard. Mint light leaks from the deep reactor chamber; a storm-blue opening far behind reveals a colossal derelict machine outside. Terrain supports grappling and banking. A compact mint reactor to defend stands ahead and to the right in the combat space, visually clear but not the foreground focus. A small group of angular orange-red robotic beetle enemies approaches along a clear lane. Loose distinct weapon pickups and scrap lie along an optional path.

Visual style: high-quality stylized real-time 3D game art direction, chunky beveled forms, expressive engineering, carefully limited texture detail, tactile painted metal, strong silhouette readability. Cute pilot contrasted with outsized mechanical destruction. Warm cream, dark petroleum blue, orange machinery and luminous mint. New epic world and strong perspective rather than the reference's tabletop framing. Restrained particles and a few clear tracers, no overwhelming VFX.
No UI, no text, no logos, no watermark. No copied characters or recognizable architecture from another game. Keep this grounded as a visually coherent future indie game, not photorealism.
