# Furnace Fleet enemy concept study

5 September 2026. Exploratory visual development for the conflict and enemy proposals in [CONFLICT-AND-COMBAT.md](CONFLICT-AND-COMBAT.md). The Furnace Fleet is the team's recommended candidate, not approved canon.

## Method and intent

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. No input reference image was supplied. This is a new raster concept sheet, not an edit of the first scene study, a runtime screenshot, a model, rig, or animation. The sheet compares two regular enemies, a miniboss, and a major boss. A small accumulated player ball supplies scale in the boss panel.

The prompt below is preserved exactly. The generated result is saved at [04-furnace-fleet-enemies.png](art/04-furnace-fleet-enemies.png).

## Visual inspection

The generated sheet shows all four requested labels and distinct silhouettes: Plowback's broad shield, Clampcart's capture jaws, Sluiceback's armored nose and valve wheels, and Counterweight Crown's hammer and track. Loose armor panels make salvage visible. A small animal is visibly inside the reference player's cockpit.

The sheet is suitable for discussing machinery and faction coherence. Its surface treatment is more weathered and industrial than the playful motion we intend; animation and character acting would need a separate study. The boss panel is a compressed design view, not a reliable height comparison or proven arena layout. Detached parts, lines and poses do not establish functional joints, telegraph timing or safe collision volumes.

This is a raster concept produced by the built-in tool. No production mesh, rig, animation, combat asset import or runtime verification was performed.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: an original enemy and boss design sheet for Scrapkeep, an indie third-person rolling scrap-ball action roguelite. This is exploratory concept art, not a gameplay screenshot.
Create one beautifully composed wide landscape concept sheet on warm light paper, four spacious panels in a two-by-two grid. Each panel shows one fully visible original machine with a strong, readable silhouette, in a purposeful attack-preparation pose. Stylized sculptural 3D illustration with painterly industrial details, exaggerated playful machinery, charming but formidable opponents. Shared Furnace Fleet visual family: old traveling industrial-city machines, fire-dark iron, aged ivory enamel, dull copper, restrained burnt-orange furnace glow, large chunky mechanical joints. Readable parts and big shapes, avoid excessive tiny greebles.
Top left, label exactly "PLOWBACK": a low four-wheeled narrow chassis carrying a huge curved split snowplow shield, narrow exposed rear engine, stabilizing feet dropping before a straight charge. Show one small detached plow plate beside it to explain salvage.
Top right, label exactly "CLAMPCART": a gangly forklift-crab machine with oversized open articulated capture jaws and a cargo cradle, preparing to steal a small deployed turret. Wide lateral weak hinges mechanically apparent. The turret is intact and visibly recoverable.
Bottom left, label exactly "SLUICEBACK": a squat aggressive drain-clearing miniboss wearing overlapping sluice gate plates, an armored shovel nose and enormous valve-wheel rear wheels, banking sideways with one loosened gate plate and an exposed hinge. Convey mass and a playful heavy ricochet, no gore.
Bottom right, label exactly "COUNTERWEIGHT CROWN": an enormous mobile foundry crane boss with low armored counterweight carriages on a curved base track and a colossal overhead furnace hammer. The accessible carriages and huge hammer must read distinctly, with a broad rolling route below. Include a small scale-reference player ball nearby: irregular collection of mismatched scrap, tires, pipes and salvaged plates around a protected glass cockpit containing a tiny cute animal pilot INSIDE. It should clearly look like an accumulated rolling ball, not a manufactured smooth mech or humanoid. Use this ball only in bottom-right as scale reference.
Each enemy should communicate its function through silhouette, posture and visible mechanisms. Small restrained leader lines may point to a hinge or open machinery but no other text beyond the four specified names and title "SCRAPKEEP / FURNACE FLEET". No existing franchise designs, logos, photoreal war imagery, graphic injury, humans, interface overlays, stat bars, frame counts, or mock claims that this is playable. Keep comfortable whitespace and polished game art direction.
```
