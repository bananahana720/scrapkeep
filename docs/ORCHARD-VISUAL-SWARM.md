## Decisions during aggregation

- Teach inward jaws versus outward feet. An opening center is an early traversal opportunity, not a promise that every ball can pass beneath every pose.
- Spreadleg braces its central frame while the legs remain articulated for the outward stroke. Its shoes do not glue themselves to the floor before moving.
- Clearance uses the designated gameplay collision envelope at maximum normal growth. Decorative scrap can wobble outside that envelope; each protrusion need not become a separate collision hazard. The encounter designer's suggestion to include all protruding art in collision is not adopted, because it conflicts with the existing dependable-envelope hypothesis.
- Keep enemy maintenance machines autonomous. A motion review's optional animal operator is not adopted as enemy canon; the expressive animal belongs to the player cockpit in this study.
- The first encounter isolates each mechanism before pairing. Later mixtures can overlap attention demands, but must retain an achievable route at the current ball size and speed.
- Visual material and mechanical checks are paper/art review only. Generated raster images do not establish a functioning rig, collision volume or readable attack at gameplay speed.

## Review result

The reviewer found no blocking contradiction in the integrated paper encounter. The final image check confirmed removal of Spreadleg's extra raised arms and restoration of Snipcart's small-cart identity and closed blades across the storyboard's top row.

Remaining art limitations are explicit: the revised storyboard narrowed the first preparation wedge; its side/front camera is not the proposed chase camera; the binding band is not traceable enough; and complete retraction, joint paths and collision clearance are not demonstrated. Irrigation is intentionally excluded from the first boss exchange. These findings remain inputs to an articulated study, not hidden claims of finished animation.

All four project images are 1536 × 1024 and match their generated-source copies by SHA-256. Forty-nine local links across the touched documents were checked with no missing targets. No gameplay code, controls, current boss names, engine or progression systems changed. No session goal, external publication or scheduled automation was created.

All three worker lanes completed; no dropouts. Behavior remains INCONCLUSIVE at evidence levels 2–3 because no playable test occurred.
