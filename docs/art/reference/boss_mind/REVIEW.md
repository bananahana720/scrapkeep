# The Mind reference sheet review

## Modelling status

Both requested sheets and one cuff variant are saved. I compared every panel visually with the supplied approved boss board. The files preserve the main design, but they do not meet the request for fully consistent orthographic construction drawings. Use the approved board to resolve conflicts. Do not trace the generated side/top views or dimension arrows as exact geometry.

The turnaround received two correction passes. The details sheet received two correction passes. The remaining faults below survived those passes. These are visual references ready for your verdict, not an approved model specification.

## Authority and interpretation

Design authority: bosses-world-boss-mind.jpg.

The other supplied boards informed finish only. The boss is represented as the giant glove and visible short cuff. I added no humanoid torso, head, legs, weapons, faction mark, company logo, tracking lens or HIRED stamp. No HIRED stamp or identifiable boss-mounted tracking lens is visible on the supplied body board. The distant background eye-like structure is not evidence for a lens on the glove.

FRONT means the numbered dorsal surface shown in the approved hero panel. BACK means the inferred palm. RIGHT SIDE means the thumb side. This naming is stated on the turnaround because anatomical front/back could otherwise mean the reverse.

## Inferred construction

1. Complete proximal wrist termination. The source crops the forearm. Main turnaround uses a short tapered cuff with a flat charcoal closure; this does not establish that the actual boss ends there.
2. Hidden cuff surfaces. Rear wraparound leather bands, final lower hem, underside closure seam, cuff taper and full circumference are inferred.
3. Palm surface. Pale leather follows the source notes; its pad arrangement, stitching, crease directions and thickness are inferred. The generated two-zone palm is more elaborate than the single continuous pad requested.
4. Finger undersides. Leather panels, flex creases and continuity around each finger are inferred from the visible caps and dorsal bands.
5. Unseen thumb underside and web. Leather coverage and the smooth connecting web are inferred. There is no separate ball joint.
6. Side and end-on volume. Finger cross-sections, hand thickness, thumb offset and occlusion order have no orthographic source. The generated projections are proposals and remain inconsistent.
7. Closed guard reverse face and hinge clearances. Visible open-lid inset and plunger come from the board, but exact lid thickness, hinge pin length, socket depth, plunger travel and cover-to-frame fit are not specified.
8. Absolute scale. Only the pilot sphere diameter is supplied. Boss height, width and depth were chosen provisionally for the drawing.
9. Alternate open cuff. Variant 1 replaces only the inferred closure concept with a hollow sleeve. The inner lining, padding, rim thickness and fabric folds are inferred. The generated lining has quilt-like folds not established by the board.
10. Wear placement. Visible wear colours follow the references, but individual patches, scratches and weave patterns are synthesized, not source-matched texture maps.

## Variant check

## Unfixed mismatches and limits

- Strict orthographic consistency and identical scale across all five views are not achieved. The side remains oblique and the top is undersized relative to front.
- Dimension arrows still do not land on exact extrema. The height line begins below the tallest tip and ends above the lowest cuff edge. Width extension lines also miss the exact outline. Labels are provisional design assumptions, not measurements recovered from the artwork.
- Cuff folds, hand-band ridges, stitch placement, pad contours, seam segmentation and wear vary between views and sheets.
- The open hatch frame and hinge are close visual approximations; their exact source shapes were not recovered. The rendered closed/open states are not mechanically fit-tested.
- The background is grey and free of scenery but has mild tonal variation and contact shadows, rather than a perfectly uniform light-grey field.
- Exact hex reproduction is not certified. Neutral-light renders still shade material colours, and generation introduced warm shifts. Use the numeric palette below for material base colours rather than sampling image pixels.
- These are generated raster illustrations, not renders of one shared 3D mesh. There is no verified joint rig, collision geometry, hidden structure or manufacturing tolerance.

## Questions for the modeller

1. What is the approved overall boss scale? Confirm it before sizing the cuff socket around the 1.84 m pilot ball.
2. Does the unseen wrist end in a cap, an open sleeve, or a continuation of the forearm? The two supplied readings do not settle that.
3. Is the inferred palm a continuous leather surface or does it have separate reinforcement pads? Confirm the seam map before UV work.
4. What is the true hand thickness and thumb web geometry? Resolve these in one shared 3D blockout, then render genuine side and top orthographic views.
5. What are the plunger travel, lid opening angle and reel-contact clearances? Keep the cuff location as the shown default.
6. Is there an approved separate tracking-lens design? The note mentions one but this board does not establish a lens on the body. Do not invent one.
7. Does phase loss change only the 04 pose/material and seam illumination, or also its articulation limits?
8. Are any HIRED stamps required on a different crew asset? None is established on this body by the supplied board.
