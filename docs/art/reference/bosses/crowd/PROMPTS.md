# Slag Bowl crowd crew: prompts

The Slag Bowl (Brindle's arena, world 1) seats 163 crew in the stands by MultiMesh from two bodies, `CrewFan` and `CrewPennant` (sketches/core-loop/tools/blender/bosses/arena_bowl_yard.py, seated by src/presentation/arena_bowl_dress.gd). Today they are recipe shapes built from stacked spheres. These sheets are the reference for one Mojulo smooth shell per body. Approved boards passed first: docs/art/reference/spillway/spillway-crews-moodboard.jpg, then spillway-combat-brindle-bowl.jpg.

## Shared preamble

You are drawing model reference sheets for Scrapkeep, a 3D action roguelite. A 3D modeller will sculpt the character from these sheets as one smooth shell, so accuracy and consistency between views matter more than drama.

Canon to follow:
- These are the Spillway crew from the first reference image: smooth pear-shaped beavers with a wide work hat and a flat paddle tail, soft-toy proportions, relaxed and friendly. Here they are spectators in the stands of a steel arena, cheering their side.
- Body: one smooth continuous shell from feet to head, like a vinyl toy: no seams, no stacked balls, no visible joints. The mitten hands, the hat brim and the tail may read as separate soft forms, but the body, head and arms flow together.
- Fur warm grey-brown #a99a84, belly and muzzle cream #c4b8a2, work hat ochre #a48f55 with a wide brim, mittens brown #8f6b4c, two small front teeth in bone #d2c5a5. Small dark eyes, no glow. Muted, non-emissive colours only. No weapons, no targeting brackets, no reward diamonds, no hazard stripes, no purple.
- All hands: a small crew number stencilled in bone on the hat band, and a tiny rectangular HIRED patch on the chest.
- The crew sits: seated on a plain bench edge, feet forward, about 1.6 m tall when seated including the hat. Front is the direction the face looks.
- Scale: draw the 1.84 m player ball (a see-through Hoberman-lattice sphere) standing on the ground line in every view, at the same scale as the front view. The body keeps its height against it in every panel.
- Sheet style: clean model sheet on a flat light grey background, even studio light, no scenery, true orthographic front, side, back and top cameras (the top looking straight down), thin dimension lines with metre labels, view names as small captions. Original design only; imitate no living artist or studio.

Make two images, saved as PNG in the current directory with the exact names given below.

## Crew fan, `crew_fan`

Spec:
- Pose: seated, both arms raised high in a cheer, mittens open above the hat, mouth open in a happy shout. Tail flat on the bench behind.
- Silhouette from 100 m across the bowl: a pear with a wide hat and two raised mittens (a Y shape).

Images:
1. `crew_fan-turnaround.png`: front, left side, back, three-quarter (front-left, from slightly above) and top views, plus the scale ball in every view. Label seated height, shoulder width and hat brim width.
2. `crew_fan-details.png`: close-ups of the face (eyes, teeth, muzzle), a mitten, the hat with its brim and stencilled number, the tail from behind, and the HIRED chest patch.

## Crew pennant, `crew_pennant`

Spec:
- Pose: seated, the right arm raised holding a thin steel pole (about 1.7 m) with a triangular cloth pennant in oxide red #94503c flying to the side; the left mitten rests on the knee. Smile.
- Silhouette from 100 m: a pear with a wide hat and a tall flag beside it.

Images:
1. `crew_pennant-turnaround.png`: front, left side, back, three-quarter (front-left, from slightly above) and top views, plus the scale ball in every view. Label seated height, pole height and pennant length.
