# Slag Bowl crowd crew reference sheets

**Purpose.** The Slag Bowl is Brindle's arena in world 1. Its stands seat 163 crew by MultiMesh from two bodies, `CrewFan` and `CrewPennant` (`sketches/core-loop/tools/blender/bosses/arena_bowl_yard.py`, seated by `src/presentation/arena_bowl_dress.gd`). Until w24 they were built from stacked spheres. Andrew ruled on 23 Oct that new critter and crew bodies are sculpted as one Mojulo smooth shell from a multi-angle reference set, so these sheets came first. The prompts are in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md). Codex `gpt-6-astra` drew them on 29 Oct from the approved boards `spillway/spillway-crews-moodboard.jpg` and `spillway/spillway-combat-brindle-bowl.jpg`, plus the palette, shape and materials style masters. Each folder's `REVIEW.md` is the generator's own check. The JPEGs here are re-encoded at quality 84.

**Modeller's check (Claude, 29 Oct).** Both sheets match the crews board: smooth pear-shaped beavers, wide work hats and flat paddle tails, with no weapons, no purple and no glow. Each sheet carries a crew number and a HIRED patch. The views are drawings, not projections, and their scale drifts against the 1.84 m ball. The model follows the written dimensions instead: about 1.6 m seated to the hat crown, a 0.9 m hat brim, and a 1.7 m pennant pole. Two choices sit below what can be read in the stands from 100 m away, so the model leaves them out: the crew number and the HIRED patch, and separate fingers on the mittens.

The shells are `bosses/crew_fan.mojulo.json` and `bosses/crew_pennant.mojulo.json` (cm, z up, facing +x, seat at z = 0), minted by `bosses/crew_shells.py`. The yard recipe joins and decimates each piece (body 1,100 triangles, hat 220, teeth 12). It paints the cream belly and muzzle, the mittens, the tail and the hat band by position on the one skin. The eyes are two small spheres, and the pennant's pole and cloth stay crisp parts.
