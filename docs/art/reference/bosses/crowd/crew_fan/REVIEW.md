## Regeneration and remaining deviations

Six turnaround generations/corrections addressed scale drift, the erroneous hat badge, raised arms, separate-looking feet, tail orientation and dimension text. Two detail generations corrected the hat hex label and removed surface grain.

- **Dimension fidelity remains incomplete.** The height and width labels are intended dimensions, not reliable measurements of the raster. The crown-to-ground / sphere ratio is still slightly too large; shoulder/brim guides do not establish exact widths. Balls are close in displayed size, not pixel-identical. Build to the stated dimensions instead of tracing this sheet as a calibrated blueprint.
- **Three-quarter is too shallow**, close to the front view. Elevation cameras also retain small illustrative projection differences. Top is visibly overhead, but all five views are not mathematically registered.
- The turnaround tail is longer and narrower than the broad paddle in the detail sheet. Use the detail sheet for paddle form; reconcile its length in the sculpt.
- Legs retain some lobe-like shaping and feet have residual brown shading. Sculpt these as continuous warm grey-brown body geometry, without separate pieces or brown footwear.
- The lattice balls communicate scale but do not establish an engineered Hoberman linkage. The 100 m silhouette has not been tested in the game.

Ready for visual review; the remaining projection and dimensional deviations prevent calling this an exact sculpting turnaround.
