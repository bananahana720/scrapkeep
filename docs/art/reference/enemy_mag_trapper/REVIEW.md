Palette, shape and material boards informed the initial finish prompt only. They were not used to add tools, machinery, symbols or luminous parts.

## What I checked

I visually inspected the attached approved board and every returned generated image. The final saved PNGs are byte-for-byte copies of the inspected generator outputs. I checked each turnaround panel for the stored stack, cab, operator, chest plaque, crane, suspended disc, feet, hoses and separate floor component. I compared disc counts and hidden construction across views, checked the alternate sheet separately, and verified the saved PNG formats and hashes.

This is visual inspection of raster illustrations. No mesh exists, and no camera, material albedo or physical dimensions were measured from a 3D model. The dimensions printed in the sheet were proposed in the prompt, not supplied by the approved board.

### Scale and dimensions

Only the pilot ball diameter, 1.84 m, is fixed by the request. The machine's 3.10 m height, 2.80 m width and 5.50 m reach are inferred working proposals. They are not approved dimensions and do not match the raster scale consistently.

The final correction explicitly requested a common scale and a smaller ball, but the image generator did not follow it. Do not use dimension-line lengths or panel silhouettes to calculate model dimensions. The width/height conflict also means that uniformly resizing whole panels would not repair the geometry.

### Markings and finish

The source has an existing dark geometric emblem on a brass square chest plaque. The generated emblem is an approximation; its central and side shapes are not a verified tracing. Use the approved image directly for that marking.

No new HIRED stamps, crew numbers, glove graphics or company marks were requested or observed on the saved machine. No mint weak point was added. The approved board does not establish one. Hazard coral was omitted with the ground warning on this neutral reference sheet; this omission does not remove the gameplay warning from the design.

The requested hex values were included in prompts. Shading and generated paint variation mean exact albedo compliance is unverified. Set the final model's material values from the user's palette rather than sampling shaded pixels. Wear is heavier and more evenly scattered than the source's localized water-wear intent. Bolt counts, individual wear patches and bracket spacing are not consistent enough to serve as texture or assembly specifications.

## Inferred construction

1. **Five stored discs for the main sheet.** The main hero panel appears to show five broad discs. The inset count is less clear. Five is a working reading, not a settled capacity.
2. **Six stored discs as an alternative.** The HOME NICHE inset can be read as six. Variant A adds one disc pitch while retaining a single stack.
3. **Four legs.** Occlusion in the board hides some feet and connections. The sheets assume one short leg at each chassis corner. The corrected top view uses four feet.
4. **Far-side leg construction.** Matching broad boots, joints and hoses were inferred from the visible side. Exact mirrored plates, bolt counts and hose entry positions are not established.
5. **Low rectangular chassis.** Hidden central chassis structure is simplified. No detailed underside, transmission or engine is established.
6. **Centred rear magazine.** The magazine is placed behind the cab on the fore-aft centreline. Its exact lateral offset is not proven by the board.
7. **Rear closure.** A plain oxide/bone bolted panel closes the unseen rear, with a simple supporting crossmember. Panel shape, bolts and mount positions are inferred.
8. **Rear hose routing.** The returned sheet adds visible rear hose bends and couplers. Their route and count are not source-confirmed.
9. **Crane root and hidden joint backs.** The visible beam, upper rod and pivot housings come from the board. Their rear faces, exact pin axes, root support and fasteners are inferred. The returned views disagree on pin orientation.
10. **Held-disc top mounting.** A central wrist boss is the simplest connection consistent with the board. Exact attachment details and unseen fasteners are inferred.
11. **Held-disc underside.** The visible concentric recess is retained. Recess depth, internal plate construction and whether the mouth has another moving part are unresolved.
12. **Open floor ring.** The main sheet assumes a through-hole, with the background visible at its centre in the detached top inset. The board does not prove whether it has a hidden bottom plate.
13. **Closed shallow floor pan.** Variant B shows the alternative bottom plate and a schematic section. Its thickness and depth are proposals, not observed facts.
14. **Floor brackets.** Their broad bolted shapes are visible in the board. The complete circumference, bracket count and spacing are inferred and drift between illustrations.
15. **Cab interior and back.** Seat, controls, rear opening and some operator coverage are inferred. Only the board's visible fox, cap, shirt and open cab are established.
16. **Dimensions and deployed pose.** All machine dimensions and exact distances between the chassis, held disc and ground ring are inferred. The illustrated pose is a static interpretation, not a validated rig.
17. **Feed latch release.** Two rejected details prompts proposed rotating the visible latch around its hinge. No image of that motion was returned. Do not treat this proposed motion as approved mechanics.

## Variant review

`enemy_mag_trapper-variant-1.png` combines two separately labelled uncertainties.

- **A, six-disc magazine.** Six discs and five gaps are visible. The adjacent carrier, hose and plates resemble the source. Extra surrounding hardware and exact bracket geometry remain inferred. The scene is a crop, not a second full-body turnaround.
- **B, shallow floor pan.** Both views show a recessed solid bottom. This distinguishes it clearly from the through-hole reading. The section is labelled inferred. Bracket placement differs between the two views and is not reliable assembly guidance.
- The panel backgrounds are light grey, but the generator added charcoal title/footer framing. This departs from the requested fully flat light-grey sheet.
- No second variant file was generated. These are independent choices; choosing six discs does not require choosing a closed floor pan.

## Questions for the modeller and art owner

1. Is the working magazine capacity five discs or six? Use the main scene's five until an owner resolves the inset discrepancy.
2. Is the floor component a through-ring, a shallow pan, or a rim around a deployed pit? Confirm its underside and collision shape.
3. How does one stored disc reach the held position? Confirm feed direction, latch motion, transfer clearance and whether the held disc becomes the floor component.
4. Which feature is the launcher mouth? Confirm whether the concentric underside of the held disc is the intended mouth or whether a separate mouth is absent from this board.
5. Is the magazine centred or offset, and is the crane base aligned with it? Resolve this before orthographic modelling.
6. What are the machine's actual width, height, reach and floor-disc diameter relative to the fixed 1.84 m ball? Current labels are proposals only.
7. Are four legs and mirrored far-side hardware correct? Confirm rear panel layout, hose routes and underside clearance.
8. Is a service hatch or weak point intended at all? None is identified in the approved board. Do not invent a mint emitter from the finish boards.
9. Keep the proposed intake-debris separator origin, or make it purpose-built Fleet anti-ball hardware? This is the board's original open question, left unresolved here.
10. Obtain a clean approved version of the existing chest emblem before texturing. Do not trace the generated approximation.
