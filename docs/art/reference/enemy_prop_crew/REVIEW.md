# Prop crew reference review

## Verdict

These are generated reference studies with unresolved construction errors. They do **not** pass the requested exact-design, common-scale orthographic turnaround requirement. Use the approved board as authority. Do not trace the delivered sheets as a consistent set of modelling blueprints.

All requested named files are saved. One optional track interpretation is included. The turnaround was generated three times, the details twice, and the variant twice. Each final image was visually inspected. Correction attempts improved the side view and access captions but did not eliminate part drift or scaling errors.

## Sources and scope

- Design authority: `enemies-dig-prop-crew-reference.jpg`.
- Finish only: the supplied palette, shape and materials boards. Their machines were not intended as geometry references.
- “Front” means the face bearing the brass plate. This coordinate convention is inferred, because the approved board does not define axes.
- The only supplied physical measurement is the pilot ball's 1.84 m diameter.
- No new crew numbers, gloves, HIRED stamps, weapons or glowing weak points were intentionally introduced. They are not visible on this body's approved board.
- No hazard footprint is included in the studio sheets. The board's floor danger graphic is not body paint.

## Inferred construction inventory

1. Front/right/back coordinate assignment and the selection of the unbraced side as “right.”
2. All machine dimensions and the roof/base aspect ratios. Only the 1.84 m pilot diameter is supplied.
3. Two continuous longitudinal crawler assemblies in the main interpretation, concealed rollers, tread return path and side armor.
4. Rear access panel layout, rear bumper/guards, rails and fastening.
5. Hidden side panel boundaries and their attachment to the chassis.
6. Solid unseen faces of mast collars, their rear seams and hidden fasteners.
7. Timber bundle seams, plank count and the unseen back of the mast.
8. Hydraulic end-mount geometry, hidden pin bearings and the concealed end of the small vertical jack.
9. The stepped internal shape suggested by the circular base collar. The board establishes its exterior, not its bearing, rotation or locking mechanism.
10. Roof top surface, slab thickness on hidden edges, fracture continuity, rear edge shape and retaining bands across the top.
11. Hidden timber deck coverage, beam continuation, beam intersections and underside fasteners.
12. Removability of an existing front cover, its four-fastener access method, recess depth and empty interior. No hinge or interior machine was established.
13. Wear on unseen surfaces and completion of partly obscured paint chips.
14. Pilot ball rear lattice and simplified hub details. This is only a scale proxy, not a separate approved pilot vehicle sheet.
15. Four short crawler pods as the alternate interpretation in the variant. This is an alternative to item 3, not an additional chassis configuration established by canon.

Exact visible bolt counts, panel seams and plaque geometry are not license to infer replacements. Where generated art varies them, that is a mismatch listed above.

## Variant review

`enemy_prop_crew-variant-1.png` explores four short crawler pods, two per side, instead of two long belts. The first version falsely presented a front-like drawing as a right-side orthographic. It was regenerated as one three-quarter view.

The final image makes separate squat drive modules more visible. It still adds or redistributes oxide panel blocks and does not expose enough of the hidden side to prove four consistent loops. Roof slabs, brackets and some small mast fittings also drift. Its upper body must not supersede the board. It is an unapproved alternate reading, not a mechanically validated solution.

No second variant was generated. Additional canopy or service mechanisms would add unsupported design choices.

## Finish checks

The sheets retain the board's oxide, charcoal, sandstone, brass and natural timber roles. They do not adopt the style boards' petrol body, mint emitter or ancient-green elements. Body coral hazard stripes, scenery and action effects are absent.

Palette hex values were specified in prompts and appear as labels on the details sheet. Rendered pixels are shaded approximations, not verified exact material swatches. Use the user's supplied hex values when authoring materials. The detail sheet's scratches and timber grain remain denser than the softer approved finish.

## Questions for the modeller before geometry lock

1. Are there two continuous crawler belts, four independent short pods, or another drive arrangement behind the armor?
2. What are the approved machine dimensions relative to the 1.84 m pilot ball? Replace the proposed measurements before scale-sensitive modelling.
3. Which canopy pieces stay attached, and which fall after the safe-side ram? Where is the intended release joint?
4. How do the diagonal brace and small vertical jack attach and move? Is the circular collar fixed or able to rotate?
5. Which visible panel is removable, and what should an open view reveal?
6. Can the original brass-plate symbol be supplied as a clean decal? Do not canonize the generated circle-centred interpretation.

The board's tactical question remains open: whether the shoring reads as an intentional ram target. These static sheets do not test gameplay readability or approve conversion from utility support into a combat unit.
