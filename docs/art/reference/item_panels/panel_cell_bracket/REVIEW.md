# Cell Bracket reference review

Both requested PNGs were generated and revised with the image generation tool. Each is 1536 × 1024 RGB. These are reviewed concept references with unresolved modelling deviations, not exact construction drawings.

## Checked

- Parts: three upright cylindrical cells, ribbed caps, round wire bracket, end clips, curved stamped backing plate, raised rim, panel seam, four mounting fasteners and one bone charge flag.
- Views: complete front and side ball views; separate face, edge, back, flag-down, flag-up and cap/clip detail views. Edge view correctly overlaps the three cells into one profile.
- Appearance: bone faces, steel edges/caps/bracket and charcoal hardware; petrol lattice and brass joints only on the ball. No glow, prohibited accent colours, characters or scenery. Exact hex values are palette targets, not sampled flat-colour guarantees under rendered lighting.
- Marks: small charcoal `047` fleet number; correct item identifier and active recharge +4% heading. No additional franchise marks. `047` is a proposed stencil, not a verified registered fleet identifier.
- Dimensions: labels show ball diameter 1.84 m, plate width 0.45 m, radius 0.92 m and projection 0.16 m, below the 0.25 m limit. Detail sheet also proposes 0.34 m plate height and 0.10 m cell diameter.
- Scale correction: final ball diameter dimension spans are approximately 675 and 670 image pixels, within about 1%. Front plate width is approximately 160 pixels, about 24% of ball width; target is 24.46%.
- Flag correction: raised flag was moved from above the cell to the right bracket clip. Both states now depict a flag associated with that clip.

## Remaining deviations

- Backing plate still encloses the cap tops. The three-cell outer silhouette and colour-independent recognition at 20 m are not established. Geometry correction attempts were rejected by the image service with an output-stage violence classification despite the mechanical subject.
- Plate outline is a clipped rectangle, not a resolved triangular/hexagonal socket fit. Ball shows an open geodesic cage, without a fully resolved Hoberman scissor mechanism or mounting interface. Its two views do not prove identical lattice topology under rotation.
- The small on-ball panel and detail sheet differ in plate aspect ratio, cap shape and bracket fittings. Use the numeric width and sphere diameter as constraints; do not trace both sheets as mutually exact projections.
- Face/edge/back are presented as orthographic, but generation does not guarantee true projection. The raised flag remains somewhat offset from the down-state pivot and changes apparent length. Hinge axis and travel need explicit resolution in the model.
- Dimension witnesses and drawn proportions are approximate. The 0.34 m height does not match the face drawing's width-to-height ratio exactly. Radius and projection are labelled design intent, not measurements of a shared 3D model. No runtime distance/readability test was performed.

Five generation/correction attempts were rejected by the image service. Successful revisions corrected the gross on-ball oversizing, brought the ball scales close together and improved the flag attachment. Original generated outputs remain in the tool's generated-images directory.
