Created with the built-in image generation tool. Both final PNGs were visually inspected at 1536 × 1024. These are modelling references, with the deviations below.

## Checked

- One horizontal cylindrical pressure tank, two steel strap bands with fixed buckles, one left-end round gauge, one right-end short pipe stub, and a curved six-sided stamped mounting plate.
- Four plate mounting bolts, raised rim, bevelled edges, panel lines, restrained enamel wear, and charcoal fleet stencil `06`. Gauge bezel screws and strap fasteners are separate from the four mounting bolts.
- Bone #d2c5a5, steel #9aa0a0 and charcoal #242a28 are the intended panel materials. No forbidden accent colours or glow are visible. Petrol lattice and brass joints occur only on the ball. Rendered lighting changes pixel values; these are material targets, not exact flat swatches.
- Face, axial edge and back views; low and high gauge states; combined dial and buckle close-up. The needle is the only moving part. The axial view was regenerated to hide the far-end pipe correctly.
- One complete on-ball view, so no conflicting ball scales across views. The final plate spans approximately one quarter of the ball diameter, consistent with 0.45 / 1.84 = 24.5%.
- Labels specify 1.84 m ball diameter, 0.45 m plate width, R 0.92 m curvature, and 0.22 m intended projection against the 0.25 m limit.
- Plain grey background, even studio light, no scenery, characters or copied insignia. Both sheets identify stored dunk damage +6%.

## Corrections and remaining deviations

Regenerated the on-ball sheet twice to reduce the oversized assembly. Regenerated details to correct the visible far-end pipe, align face/back outlines, and remove unsupported optional dimensions. One annotation edit received an erroneous violence rejection; the identical retry succeeded.

The on-ball diameter witness lines still stop inside the sphere's extreme top and bottom. Its panel width arrow still follows the narrower upper edge rather than the widest outline. The edge-view projection leader still terminates near a bolt rather than precisely on the sphere datum. Use the written dimensions above, not those arrow endpoints.

Main detail views read as orthographic, but generated raster art cannot certify camera matrices or exact geometry. Minor buckle, bolt and gauge proportions vary between sheets; use the details sheet to build those parts. The lattice illustrates the mounting context, not verified Hoberman linkage mechanics or a validated socket attachment.

No 3D clearance, animation or 20 m gameplay readability test was performed. Build to the stated dimensions and check the tank silhouette in-game before accepting the asset.
