## Remaining deviations

- The ball is a simplified jointed geodesic cage. It does not faithfully resolve the crossed links and kinematics of a Hoberman lattice.
- Generated views are not exact CAD projections. Some depth cues remain, and plate curvature, proportions and bolt visibility vary slightly between views.
- On-ball dimension extension lines still miss some intended endpoints, especially the fore-aft span. Pixel proportions do not precisely reproduce all labels. Build to the dimension table, not by tracing those lines.
- The small on-ball stencil is softened. Four mounting bolts are explicit in the face/back views but partly hidden in installation views.
- Colour-independent recognition at 20 m has not been tested in a game camera. These are reference drafts for review, not verified production geometry.
