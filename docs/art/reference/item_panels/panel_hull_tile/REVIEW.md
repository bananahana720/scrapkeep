# Hull Tile reference review

Both requested PNGs were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and visually inspected after each revision. Each final file is 1536 × 1024. These are model concept references with the dimensional deviations below, not exact construction drawings.

## Checked

- Parts: six-sided armour plate, integral raised plus-shaped cross-brace, heavy steel bevelled rim, recessed seams, four front bolts, four rear fixing positions. No moving parts. Face, edge section, back, fresh, dented and bevel close-up are present.
- Matching design: corrected the initial on-ball octagonal outline and steel brace to the hexagonal outline and bone brace used in the detail sheet. The mounted group contains six tiles.
- Palette: bone face, steel edges, charcoal recesses and front bolt heads. Petrol lattice and brass joints appear only on the ball. No prohibited accent colours or glow were observed. Exact base-colour hex values cannot be confirmed from shaded pixels.
- Marks: panel_hull_tile, MAX HEALTH +5 and F-05 are present. F-05 is an original placeholder fleet number; no additional canon emblem was supplied or invented.
- Scale: both bare balls occupy approximately 690 pixels across and are labelled 1.84 m. After regeneration, the single mounted tile is approximately 170 pixels wide, consistent with 0.45 / 1.84 = 24.46%. Repeated tiles are smaller in projection; their exact surface placement is illustrative.
- Dimensions: 0.45 m width, 0.39 m height, R 0.92 m seat, 0.035 m body thickness, 0.060 m installed projection and 0.008 m bevel are labelled. Projection is below the requested 0.25 m limit. Height, thickness, projection and bevel are proposed modelling dimensions chosen for this sheet.
- Presentation: flat grey background, studio light, no scenery or characters. Face/back are frontal orthographic-style views; edge is a side section. The bevel detail is intentionally an oblique close-up.

## Remaining deviations

- Generated outlines and dimension endpoints are not pixel-exact: the detail face is slightly wider relative to height than a regular 0.45 × 0.39 m hexagon, and its width arrows stop slightly inside its tips. Use the written dimensions to construct the mesh, not tracing.
- The regenerated edge section now shows a curved shell, but exaggerates curvature and retains a dashed seat arc bending the wrong way. Construct the mounting surface mathematically at R 0.92 m; do not trace that dashed arc. The rear stamping also remains visually ambiguous as raised versus recessed.
- “Fresh” means undented here; it still has minor enamel wear. Rear nuts are lighter than the requested charcoal bolts.
- The lattice is a geodesic visual stand-in; the images do not establish a functioning Hoberman linkage or the four-bolt/socket interface. Twenty-metre monochrome readability and in-game fit have not been tested.
