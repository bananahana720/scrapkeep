# Punch Rivet reference review

Both requested PNGs were made and revised with the built-in image generation tool. Final sheets were visually inspected; PNG integrity and hashes are recorded in `IMAGE_CHECKS.json`. Prompts are in `GENERATION_PROMPTS.md`.

## Checked

- Parts: one central domed head, eight small surrounding rivets and four corner mounting bolts in both face depictions. The final on-ball correction restored a missing eighth rivet.
- Details: face, edge, back, rested, pushed-out and spring cutaway are present. The section labels the head, shaft, coil spring, guide barrel, curved plate and bolt as ordinary machine parts.
- Finish: bone face, steel edges/head/rivets, charcoal recesses/bolts and stencil `017`. Petrol and brass are confined to the ball. No glow, forbidden accent colours, characters, scenery or invented fleet emblem. The supplied references establish style; `017` is an illustrative fleet number, not a verified registered canon number.
- Dimensions: labels specify a 1.84 m ball, 0.45 m plate, 0.18 m head and 0.04 m outward travel. Additional design assumptions are R 0.92 m curvature, 0.025 m plate thickness, 0.07 m dome rise and 0.11/0.15 m rested/pushed projection, below the 0.25 m limit.
- Presentation: neutral grey studio background, small view captions and metre dimensions. One full-ball view is retained; discarded two-ball versions had inconsistent apparent sphere widths and oversized plates.

## Remaining deviations

- These remain generated modelling references, not exact orthographic construction drawings. Use the written dimensions when building; do not trace pixel proportions. The on-ball plate is approximately 23% of sphere width rather than the intended 24.5%. The detail-face head is slightly undersized relative to its plate, and the pushed-state extension is visually exaggerated.
- Some dimension extension lines still miss their intended surfaces despite regeneration, especially the sphere poles and dome-rise arrow. Corner shape and curvature vary slightly between sheets; use the clipped square outline on the detail face as the shape reference.
- The open lattice conveys the ball's appearance but does not prove a functional Hoberman linkage or an exact triangular-hexagonal socket fit. Four-point attachment brackets and spring retention need resolution in the 3D model. True orthographic camera setup cannot be verified from these raster images.
- Palette compliance was checked visually against the specified roles; lighting and wear mean rendered pixels are not exact hex values. Silhouette readability at 20 m has not been tested in-game.

Status: ready for visual review, with the dimensional and fit limitations above still open.
