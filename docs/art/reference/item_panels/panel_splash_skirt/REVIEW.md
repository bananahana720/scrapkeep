# Splash Skirt review

Saved two 1536 × 1024 RGB PNGs using the built-in image generation tool. Visually inspected both initial sheets and both corrective generations. Final prompts are in `GENERATION_PROMPTS.md`.

## Checked

- **Parts:** chamfered stamped mounting plate, raised rim, steel edge, four charcoal mounting bolts, three scalloped hinged lip segments, hinge leaves, pin and stop. The back shows four corresponding fasteners. The lip assembly is the moving part.
- **Views:** full-ball single-panel placement and repeated equatorial ring; detail face, edge, back, folded lip, fanned lip and segment-hinge close-up. Face and back use frontal orthographic presentation; edge uses side presentation.
- **Scale:** both complete ball bodies have matching apparent diameters and separate 1.84 m dimensions. Panel width is labelled 0.45 m and is approximately one quarter of ball diameter. Detail height is labelled 0.30 m; mounting radius is 0.92 m. The 0.12 m folded and 0.15 m fanned projections remain below the 0.25 m limit. The +0.35 m stat is labelled separately from physical dimensions.
- **Colours:** bone face, steel edges and charcoal recesses/bolt heads; petrol lattice and brass joints confined to the ball. No glow or prohibited accent colours observed. Hex values were supplied to the generator; rendered shading is not a flat-colour swatch guarantee.
- **Marks and presentation:** consistent F-017 stencil, no added logos, characters or scenery. Plain light grey background, even lighting and small view captions. Flared three-scallop silhouette retained.
- **Corrections:** regenerated the detail sheet to repair side-view dimension witnesses and angle annotation; regenerated the mounted sheet to add the second diameter label, reduce panel size and show explicit scissor-link pivots.

## Remaining deviations

- Folded and fanned detail views still differ slightly in apparent lip width despite their equal 0.45 m labels. Use a single rigid set of three segments when modelling; do not reproduce that width change.
- The side profile, drawn angle and hinge inset remain illustrative rather than exact engineering projections. Use the written 35° fanned / 48° folded angles and dimension targets, then reconcile the hinge and sphere curvature in CAD or Blender. The 0.03 m caption describes the outward projection increase, not the total arc travelled by the tip.
- The ball shows scissor pivots but is not a verified expandable Hoberman mechanism. Exact socket fit, hinge clearance, maximum projection and 20 m silhouette readability need the built model and game camera. This review does not establish those results.
