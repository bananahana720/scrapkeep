## Remaining deviations and modelling guidance

- These are visual references, not dimensionally exact engineering drawings. Use the stated metre dimensions when modelling. Some dimension extension lines end inboard of the actual silhouette; the on-ball projection annotation is schematic.
- The two ball silhouettes differ in size by roughly 2%, despite equal-scale instructions. Their lattice topology also varies between views. The open petrol/brass cage conveys the setting but does not reliably depict a folding Hoberman scissor mechanism; use the project's actual ball mesh for fitting.
- Pawl and tooth profiles vary slightly between views. The back view depicts solid hexagonal fasteners rather than clearly resolved nuts. Establish one consistent axle, pawl and mounting arrangement in the model.
- Curvature, bolt alignment, moving-part clearances, and the 0.20 m projection have not been verified in 3D. Exact colour values and recognition from 20 m require an in-game check; neither is established by these sheets.
