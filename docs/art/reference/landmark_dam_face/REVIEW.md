# Dam-face waterworks reference review

## Status

The requested two sheets and one ambiguity variant are saved. I inspected every rendered view against the approved board and made two turnaround corrections, one details correction, and one variant correction.

These are visual reference drafts. The turnaround does not meet the requested exact same-scale orthographic standard. Do not trace its projections or derive dimensions from its pixels. Its five views preserve the main assembly, but projection, scale and small-part consistency remain unresolved. The approved board and authored dimensions take precedence.

## Authority and dimensions

Design source: spillway-landmarks-dam-face.jpg. The other three supplied boards informed finish only.

| Authored measurement | Meaning and limit |
| --- | --- |
| 320 m crest | Length along the crest curve. It is not a straight chord width. |
| Approximately 120 m face | Crest-to-toe height for layout. Exact end and center elevations are not established by the board. |
| Reactor 22 m above toe | Source wording does not identify the measured point on the reactor. Prompts assumed the mounting datum; that assumption needs confirmation. |
| Pilot ball diameter 1.84 m | Must be 1.533% of a 120 m face height in a true shared-scale elevation. The saved drawing does not achieve this ratio. |

No thickness, plan radius, spillway width, tower height, shelf dimensions or reactor dimensions were supplied. Those proportions remain illustrative.

The source shows a green reactor seam and beam. I preserved its acid-green appearance, using #9dff3c from the palette board's ancient role. This colour is outside the user's enumerated body palette, so it is a documented source-preservation choice, not a newly approved palette exception. No mint weak point, coral body paint, logos, crew numbers, gloves or HIRED stamps were invented.

## Inferred construction inventory

1. Overall hidden concrete mass, upstream wall slope, thickness, top walkway depth and toe footprint.
2. Crest radius and plan curvature away from the visible perspective.
3. Unseen outer end closures, including their plain rectangular treatment after extra steps were removed.
4. Tower back and side panels, roof outlines, cap seams, pole bases and the backs of the circular-port housings.
5. Exact tower symmetry and alignment, which the oblique board cannot establish.
6. Truss rear/depth members, connections and bracing intersections.
7. Gate leaves behind water, vertical guide channels, hidden gate backs, guide bolts and section depth.
8. The full route, mounting and termination of the pipe beside the source's right tower.
9. Reactor rear surface, small shell segment boundaries, crown fitting and seam interruptions.
10. Reactor plinth underside, concrete attachment and support geometry obscured by water.
11. Removable reactor side plate, extraction direction and dark empty service cavity. No mechanism inside the cavity is asserted.
12. Shelf undersides, slab thickness, braces, wall anchors, rail connections and pole footings.
13. Canopy cloth seams, rear tie points and the two-separate-cloths reading used in the primary sheets.
14. Any source-obscured extension of the crest railing, repair joints and mineral bands.
15. Water continuity behind the reactor and across the toe. Water is an effect, not part of the structural mesh.
16. Ball lattice topology at this tiny scale. Its 1.84 m diameter is authored, but the drawing's diameter is wrong.

No unseen room, turbine, power generator, weapon, gear train, faction identity or interior equipment is intentionally specified.

## Ambiguity variant

landmark_dam_face-variant-1.png compares two readings of the right shelf's canvas:

- A: two separate pointed awnings with a gap, the interpretation used in the primary sheets.
- B: one continuous cloth draped over two peaks.

The distant source cannot resolve the cloth seam. The corrected variant removes invented tall concrete end pillars. The wall behind each closeup is a cropped context patch, not a proposed freestanding wall.

Residual mismatch: support poles and rail segments are not pixel-identical between A and B. Compare the cloth connection only; do not treat the changed pole count as a second proposal. The shared balcony dimensions and anchors remain inferred.

One variant was made. Other unseen construction is documented above instead of creating competing dam designs.

## Generation and verification record

Built-in image_gen was used for all images. PROMPTS.md contains exact generation and correction prompts under the final filenames, plus input ordering and selected source filenames.

All generated candidates were visually inspected as returned. Final files are unchanged copies of the selected outputs, so copying did not require another image inspection. No CAD model or registered 3D cameras were used; no exact orthographic, geometry or pixel-colour verification is claimed.

The first file-check attempt could not import Pillow. I used Python's standard library to check PNG signatures, every chunk CRC and decompression of image data. All three passed. This verifies file integrity only.
