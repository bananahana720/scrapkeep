# Hanging Isle reference-sheet review

## Result

The requested PNG files and prompt record are saved. These sheets are review drafts, not an exact modelling master. Visual comparison found remaining changes to the rock geometry, hand construction, projection and scale. Four turnaround correction passes and one detail correction pass improved the results but did not satisfy the exact-match requirement.

Use image 1 as the design authority. Do not trace the generated dimension lines or resolve disagreements between views by averaging them. Establish one 3D blockout against the approved front silhouette before committing to the hidden surfaces.

## Sources and scope

- Approved board: `highdraft-landmark-hanging-isle.jpg`.
- Finish references: `style-palette-moodboard.jpg`, `style-shape-moodboard.jpg`, and `style-materials-moodboard.jpg`.
- The user-supplied board and notes take precedence over the generated sheets. The cited DECISIONS and LANDMARKS documents were not available in this task directory and were not independently consulted.
- The island, its short tip tether, lowered hand and hanging lens form one assembly. The foreground caretaker net belongs to separate cliff anchors. The board does not show rim suspension cables. The task's description of an island suspended from cables therefore remains an open interpretation.
- Surrounding cliffs, sky, distant mountains and approach platforms are omitted. The details show a representative detached section of caretaker rigging, not the full foreground installation.

## Inferred construction register

These are proposed readings only:

1. A roughly circular crown plan. Its actual depth is not visible.
2. Continuation of sandstone strata over the hidden right and rear surfaces.
3. Hidden shelf shapes, rock cracks and local bevels.
4. Hidden tree, shrub and rock placement across the crown.
5. A short surface runnel feeding the visible waterfall. Its route and source are unknown.
6. Central beam footprint and the shape of the natural rock beneath it.
7. Treating the ancient beam as a narrow channel-like feature rather than proving it is solid or optical.
8. Approximate ancient geometric motif. No readable alphabet is established.
9. Lower brace depth, rear continuation, fasteners and attachment sockets.
10. End-cap thickness and the tether attachment inside it.
11. Linked tether construction for the main sheets, including its link count and end fittings.
12. Continuous cable and crimped ferrules for variant 1, offered instead of the linked tether.
13. Four prominent finger rays, interpreted from two visible rays on each side. Hidden digits are unresolved.
14. Palm depth, housing seams, joint axes, collars and finger segment lengths.
15. Lens thickness, rear cover, mounting stem and optical construction.
16. The detached two-post net section, its extent and stone feet used to isolate it from scenery.
17. Hidden post faces, axle ends, caps, rope terminations and cloth ties.
18. Caretaker anatomy and garment portions hidden in the source. The contextual figures are not approved creature turnarounds.
19. All island dimensions except the user-specified 1.84 m pilot-ball diameter.

## Colour and marking checks

The images retain sandstone rock, oxide lower band, petrol machinery, charcoal joints, ochre/brass braces, pale cloth and copper accents. The prompts included the requested hex targets. Rendered lighting and generation do not guarantee exact pixel colours; material base colours must be assigned numerically in the 3D asset.

The board visibly uses ancient lime on the beam and lens. The finish palette identifies that role as `#9dff3c`; it was retained instead of changing the lens to mint. This is an explicit extension beyond the shortened hex list in the task. Mint remains reserved for the pilot ball's visible hub and is not newly assigned to the landmark as a weak point.

No legible crew number, HIRED stamp, glove emblem or faction logo is visible on the source landmark or rigging. None was added. The generated ancient line motifs should not be treated as an approved new emblem.

The generated sheets still use warmer stone and more saturated ochre than a neutral base-colour chart. Some subtle background gradients, shadows and isolated stone feet remain despite the flat-background request.

## Alternative checked

`landmark_hanging_isle-variant-1.png` shows a continuous dark cable with ferrules instead of separate tether links. It retains the tip-to-palm-to-lens order and explicitly labels itself as an alternate reading. It does not authorize crown suspension cables.

The variant also drifts in palm depth, lens size, brace fasteners and tip shape. Use it only to compare cable versus chain. It is not a replacement geometry reference for the surrounding parts. No second variant was made.

## Questions to settle before modelling

1. Does the island float independently, with only the hand tethered below it, or are unseen external support cables intended?
2. Is the short tether a chain, cable or articulated rod? Which end swivels?
3. How many fingers does the ancient hand have, and what are its actual joint axes and articulation limits?
4. Is the hanging lens directional, and does any gameplay state expose a weak point or service opening?
5. What are the island's actual crown width, depth and rock height relative to the 1.84 m pilot ball?
6. Where does the waterfall originate, and does its source move with the island?
7. Is the vertical ancient feature a light beam or solid structure? What is the approved seam pattern?
8. Which caretaker alcoves and net anchors must be included in the landmark asset? The visible board does not establish their hidden layout.

## Generation and file checks

The built-in image generation tool produced five turnaround candidates, two detail candidates and one tether alternative. Earlier rejected candidates are in `.attempts/`. `PROMPTS.md` contains all eight exact prompts in call order under their final output names.

All final images are 1536 x 1024 RGB PNGs. PNG signatures, chunk checksums and compressed image data were verified. The first metadata check could not import Pillow; a Python standard-library PNG check succeeded. This check establishes file integrity only.

Visual review used each generated result as displayed, compared with the supplied board. There was no 3D reconstruction, projection solve, topology validation or human acceptance. The directory is not a usable Git repository, so no commit was made.
