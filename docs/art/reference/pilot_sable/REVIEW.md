# Sable reference-sheet review

## Result

The requested files are saved, but the exact-design and projection checks are not fully satisfied. Use these sheets for visual discussion alongside the approved board. Do not trace them as dimensionally consistent modelling blueprints. The approved board wins wherever they differ.

The turnaround received three correction passes after its initial generation. The details sheet received one correction pass. One variant sheet shows two independent alternative readings. Remaining differences are listed below rather than treated as approved design.

All three deliverable PNGs are 1536 × 1024. PROMPTS.md contains the verbatim prompts and reference order for every final and retained intermediate image. The built-in image_gen tool generated all pixels. No programmatic repainting, cropping or compositing was used.

## Inferred parts and construction

These are proposals or generated continuations, not additional approved canon:

1. Rear lattice links, hidden crossing order, pivot axes, bushings and bolt arrangement. The visible scissor language was continued around the sphere.
2. Rear equatorial belt segments, seams, clamps, fasteners and equipment mounting faces. Plain segmented panels were chosen.
3. Seat-back outer casing, back cushion divisions, rear bolts and seat attachment.
4. Hidden gimbal arcs, bearings and their attachment to the seat and belt. The top-view transverse member is an inferred reading of the upper hoop/support.
5. Rear and side scrap patch shapes, materials, bolts and exact locations.
6. Lower belly supports, fabrication-space enclosure, under-seat clearances and their joins to the belt.
7. Small rear/hidden mast mounting faces and wire termination. The source proves the mast, not these attachments.
8. Hidden winch bracket faces, axle end treatment and cable route. The hook throat remains ambiguous.
9. Tool backs, cable connections, guard return bends, coil counts and mounting fasteners not clearly visible in the source.
10. Fox anatomy behind the seat, concealed body/clothing surfaces and harness routing. No external tail was added; a hidden tail's shape and accommodation are undetermined.
11. Unreadable cap-patch silhouette. Its pale worn appearance is retained without assigning an identity.
12. Closed sorting-channel cover plates and their removable bolt-on attachment in the main details interpretation. The source only proposes the internal layout.
13. The sorting cavity's hidden walls, depth and access clearance. No functioning sorting or fabrication mechanism was established.
14. Exact cable-guide section and attachment to the retained mount. Its proposed role comes from the board; its buildable geometry does not.
15. The alternative narrow hook throat and the two rear-edge cover hinges in the variant sheet. The hinges are entirely inferred and are not installed in the main design.
16. Sable's absolute outer-sphere diameter of 1.84 m. Only the separate comparison ball has a user-specified diameter.

## Variant

`pilot_sable-variant-1.png` contains two independent alternatives:

- A compact narrow-throat hook, closer to the board's teardrop silhouette than the wide J hook in the details sheet. The enlarged opening is still an interpretation, and its shape does not match the main sheet exactly.
- Hinged access to the proposed sorting channel instead of removable cover plates. The sheet explicitly marks the hinges as inferred and the fabrication layout as not selected.

The alternative cover opening is illustrated, but the channel's perimeter fasteners and panel arrangement vary between its two states. It is not proof that either state is buildable. The variant also simplifies the outlet guide and intake. Do not copy its inferred hardware into the approved vehicle without a design decision.

## Remaining mismatches

- Cross-view lattice topology, link count, joint connections and patch placement drift. No common 3D model generated these views.
- Front, side and top are visual approximations of orthographic views. Exact camera projection and image registration were not established.
- The five body views and comparison ball do not maintain an exact common pixel scale.
- Tool noses, guards and coil lengths vary between the turnaround, details and source.
- Hook geometry differs between the turnaround, details and variant.
- The turnaround still appears to contain a narrow copper-coloured chest crosspiece; the details correction establishes leather there. Follow the original brown harness in the approved board.
- Gimbal thickness, control-bank layout, yoke fittings, pedal shapes, seat details and small bolts are simplified or changed.
- The pilot's face, head angle, cap patch and clothing folds are not identical across views.
- The proposed intake and fabrication path are not consistently located or exposed in the whole-body turnaround.
- The closed/open fabrication illustrations change panel arrangement rather than proving one cover's motion.
- Backdrop is neutral grey but has lighting/shadow variation rather than perfectly flat light grey.
- The prompts specify the requested hex palette. Shaded generated pixels are not verified material base colours. Use the supplied hexes directly when authoring materials. There is no hazard-coral body paint or ancient-lime emission; visible mint is confined to the tool terminals after correction.

## Questions for the modeller

1. Is Sable's lattice sphere actually 1.84 m across, or is that dimension only for the comparison ball?
2. Which approved lattice topology and deployment state should all views share? A common blockout is needed before tracing joints.
3. What are the two gimbal axes, resting orientations, bearings and clearances?
4. Does "right-hand control bank" mean pilot's anatomical right or the depicted image-right bank?
5. Is the bronze fitting an open hook with a narrow throat, a wider J hook or a closed/captive eye?
6. Is the low intake/sorting/fabrication layout approved for this body at all? The source calls it proposed.
7. If that layout is selected, where does its inlet sit on the whole vehicle, how does the cover open and where is the formed guide retained?
8. What is the cap patch, and should it remain unreadable? No crew number or HIRED placement is established here.
9. What should occupy the unseen rear/underside, and where does the fox's tail fit without changing the silhouette?
10. Does the added enclosure preserve Sable's compact, quiet-machine character? The source leaves this question open.

## Evidence and file checks

I inspected each generated sheet when the image tool displayed it. I compared the listed views and details with the supplied approved image. This was visual review, not CAD registration, mechanical validation or owner acceptance.

All seven retained PNGs passed signature, chunk-CRC and compressed-image-data checks. The initial Pillow-based metadata check could not run because Pillow is not installed in the shell interpreter. A Python standard-library PNG check provided the integrity evidence instead; no dependency was installed.

Final hashes and the full image ledger are in `.review/image-integrity.json`. Rejected intermediate images remain under `.review/` so the correction sequence is inspectable. This directory is not a Git repository, so no commit was created.
