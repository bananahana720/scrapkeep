# Tally reference review

## Verdict

The requested images are saved, but the turnaround does not meet the strict same-scale, exact-projection modelling requirement. Use the approved board as authority. These sheets are ready for visual review, not approval as an exact blueprint.

Checked the displayed final generations against supplied image 1, panel by panel. No underlying shared 3D model or camera calibration exists. Exact silhouette registration, part topology and colour matching are not verified. The palette, shape and materials boards supplied finish guidance only.

## Source decisions

- The close-up explicitly shows three fitted cards, with the centre selected and raised. Primary sheets follow that close-up.
- The main source view shows two protruding tabs. The variant explores a different card state with a third card lowered, without claiming that mechanism is confirmed.
- Mulberry enamel follows the supplied board. The note about an absent approved tint does not establish an exact mulberry value.
- The supplied 1.84 m pilot ball is the scale reference. Giving Tally's lattice that same diameter is an inference, not a measurement from the board.
- No visible source crew number, HIRED stamp, faction emblem or glove emblem was legible enough to copy. None was invented.
- The board defines the card exit as the sole light. It does not identify a weak point or an opening service hatch.

## Variant review

`pilot_tally-variant-1.png` shows two protruding cards and an adjacent third flush slot. It is an alternate state study, not a replacement design.

The initial variant invented punched holes in the cream belt. A correction removed those holes and narrowed the front-plate slots. The saved variant keeps solid belt panels. Residual differences include oversized carriage/fasteners, changed card-hole spacing, and an inferred right-hand third socket. Whether the flush slot contains a card is not visually conclusive. Use this image only to discuss the two-visible-tab reading.
