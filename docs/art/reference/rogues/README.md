# Rogue body reference sheets

**Purpose.** Andrew asked on 23 Oct for multi-angle references before any rogue body is modelled. Each of the three rogue mini-bosses (`sketches/core-loop/design-v3/rogues/BRIEFS.md`) has a turnaround sheet (front, left, back, three-quarter, top, with a 1.84 m pilot ball and metre callouts) and a details sheet (governor, cut command line, hatch closed and open, exploded moving parts with bone names). The prompts are in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md). Codex `gpt-6-astra` drew them on 23 Oct with the approved boards `hierarchy/hierarchy-rogues-moodboard.jpg` and `hierarchy/hierarchy-rogue-sky-cable-tender-reference.jpg` plus the style masters as references. Each folder's `REVIEW.md` is the generator's own check, and its PNG hashes refer to the full-size originals in `~/scratch/w18/rogue-refs`. The JPEGs here are re-encoded at quality 84.

**Modeller's check (Claude, 23 Oct).** All six sheets carry the canon from the brief: there's no purple, the cut command line is oxide with frayed copper, there's a brass flyball governor, the crew number is large in bone with a HIRED stamp, and the hatch is two slate plates over a mint core. The machines are petrol enamel with brass or copper fittings, which matches the ART-MISTAKES row of 22 Oct for #251. The views are drawings, not projections, so they disagree in small ways. The model follows the written dimensions in BRIEFS.md and resolves those disagreements as follows:

| Rogue | Resolution used for the model |
|---|---|
| Gate Crane No. 14 | The counterweight drum sits behind the mast top with its axis fore-aft, and its hatch faces back. The jib yaws about a vertical axis at the mast crown. The cab is at the front-left of the mast foot, and the cut line hangs from it. The governor is on the mast crown behind the jib root. The front view's leaf hides the stencil, so the stencil goes on both sides of the mast. |
| Haul Skip No. 31 | The hoist frame arches over the rear of the bucket, not the whole tub, as all views draw it. The governor and the crushed route receiver are on the left rear upright. The hatch and the hook ring are on the rear of the chassis under the bucket lip. The bucket tips forward about a front axle. |
| Cable Tender No. 7 | The spool axle runs left-right. The hatch is on the left flange, which faces the camera in the side view. The boom pivots on a block in front of the spool on the base's centre line and reaches 5 m forward to the harpoon. The governor is on the right A-frame, as in the front view. |
