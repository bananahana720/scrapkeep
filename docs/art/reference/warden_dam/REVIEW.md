# Warden dam review

## Result

Saved the turnaround, details and one cover-motion variant as PNGs. Each is 1536 × 1024 pixels. I visually compared every generated panel with the supplied approved board. The main part inventory survives, but the sheets do not meet an exact, mutually consistent orthographic modelling standard. Use the approved board to resolve conflicts. Do not trace these sheets as calibrated construction drawings.

The turnaround was generated three times. The details sheet was generated twice. The first correction did not fix the turnaround projection. The final regeneration improved the ball size and hid the core from the top view, but did not fully fix scale or projection. The details correction removed added bumper blocks. Remaining errors are listed below, rather than marked as accepted.

### Scale and dimension check

The pilot cage is intended to be 1.84 m in diameter. Final cage size is much closer to one third of the front Warden width than in the first pass. However, the diameter dimension endpoints extend toward the reel/tool span instead of cleanly touching the cage diameter. Do not use those endpoints to derive scale.

The 5.52 m Warden width and 2.80 m depth are proposed working dimensions, not measurements supplied by the board. Perspective and different distances in the board prevent a reliable exact body-to-ball ratio. The original 5.00 m total-height guess was removed from the final prompt and sheet.

The final top view is larger than the front view, and its width/depth proportion does not precisely implement 5.52/2.80. Build one proportional blockout, then render orthographic views from that geometry before setting production dimensions. The drawn dimension labels do not prove geometric consistency.

## Inferred construction

These are the construction choices used or implied by the sheets. None overrides a visible feature in the approved board.

1. A closed rounded rear shell, with no rear service port, connector or additional machinery. The rear is not visible in the board.
2. Rear panel seams continuing the shell's front segmentation. Their exact paths and count are not established.
3. A flattened body depth of about 2.80 m. Front-to-back proportions remain provisional.
4. A total width of about 5.52 m. The scale ball is canon-sized; this body dimension is an estimate.
5. The three upper cylinders form one straight left-right row with similar height and shared cap construction. Their exact fore-aft offsets are unknown.
6. Solid circular tower lids and full circumferential top/foot collars. The board suggests closed caps but does not show complete overhead surfaces.
7. Each side cheek continues rearward as a solid thick clamp housing. Its rear attachment face, mounting contact and dam curvature fit are unresolved.
8. Small dark bearing recesses, collar depths and hinge-block surfaces inside the cheek gaps. Only their visible front appearance is evidenced.
9. The core shaft continues horizontally into the side walls of the cavity. Its concealed supports, bearings and travel are not shown.
10. A one-piece removable cover is the simplest working reading of the CLOSED/OPEN endpoints. The main sheets depict those endpoints without asserting a removal mechanism.
11. The variant explores a one-piece cover lifting upward behind the upper front shell. The travel path, pocket and internal clearance are not established. No rails, hinge or motor were specified.
12. Rear and hidden-side wear placement. Generated front wear also drifts and must not be treated as an exact copy.
13. The top silhouette and inferred underside closure. The underside was not separately drawn or verified, and no feet should be inferred from ground shadows or cheek tips.

The prompt requested simple rear mounting faces, but the output does not communicate them sufficiently. No unseen mounting system was verified.

## Questions for the modeller and art owner

1. Is the cover removable, internally lifted, or moved by another mechanism? Where does the full-size cover fit when the core is open?
2. What are the approved body width, height and depth relative to the 1.84 m pilot cage?
3. Where do the two side clamps contact and fasten to the curved dam? Does the body follow the dam slope or stay vertical?
4. What are the exact rear shell, rear seams, underside and tower-cap shapes?
5. Should the core collar read as muted steel with a yellowed edge, as the board suggests, rather than the brighter brass in these sheets?
6. What is the exact reel contact surface? May contact contribute posture while the water-sheet ram alone shatters?

Resolve dimensions and mount/cover clearance in a shared 3D blockout. Then derive all views from that model. The current images provide appearance and part references, not proof that those mechanisms fit.
