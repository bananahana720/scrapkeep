# Section 2 decisions for review

## Creative choices awaiting Andrew

- The candidate history uses accumulated repairs, abandoned infrastructure and changing routes rather than a single universal apocalypse. This gives each ruin a local cause and keeps living communities central.
- The Furnace Fleet's growing maintenance burden motivates its requisitions. Admiral Brindle believes central collection will solve shortages, while each acquisition increases what he must maintain. This adapts earlier sincere-but-wrong characterization without choosing a finale, mandatory route or fixed introduction sequence.
- Engineering cultures exchange techniques and disagree over practical construction choices. They do not own exclusive biomes or prescribe species personalities. Sable, Kest and Tarn interpret their traditions; they do not stand for every member.
- Moss appears only as a supporting workshop keeper. The older log-author role, exact species, interface ownership and introduction schedule remain unselected.
