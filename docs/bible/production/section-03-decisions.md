# Section 3 decisions and source conflicts

## Concrete review choices

- These five areas are comparison candidates. Selecting the launch catalogue, repeats, starting world or finale remains a later publisher decision. The overview deliberately uses separate samples with no arrows, numbering, compass or distance.
- Each page proposes distinct terrain, vegetation, local work and reward vocabulary. Effects, handling changes and boss consequences are creative possibilities, not selected systems. No item prices, clearance ratios, timers, forced transformation rules or new endpoints were adopted.
- The map needs a dune-specific correction if chosen for regeneration: Pale Basin is rendered as white sculpted cliffs/mesas. The M05 scene better expresses the intended broad white dunes, though its material can read as snow.
- M04 has the clearest mandatory-direction conflict: the pilot appears exposed atop the ball. A cohesion-selected correction should enclose that pilot in a protected readable cockpit while preserving the forested mountain landscape.
- M01 and M05 foreground mechs look like wheeled/treaded vehicles, weakening irregular rolling accumulation. M03 has a relatively coherent shell. The coordinator should compare these against character sheets and the final player silhouette before choosing any single regeneration. No image was regenerated here.
- All scenes trend toward detailed realistic miniature rendering instead of distinctly simplified chunky construction. Region differentiation is strong, but cross-section style review should decide whether this is an acceptable landscape-study treatment.

## Source gaps and conflicts resolved in wording

- Older world kits label Yard Commons as a home/tutorial world and describe rungs, an orange-heavy palette, specific movement numbers, mass-gated rails and mandatory progression. The approved art lock and current brief supersede those details. No old fixed order or numerical system was carried into this section.
- The Iron Orchard's bounded encounter and implemented Pruner arm are distinguished from a proposed full region and complete Grand Pruner boss. Generated M03 is concept art, not a capture of those implementations.
- Pale Basin is a new fictional geography proposal. Gypsum-like describes the visual family; no real-world location, mineral survey, new proper name or astronomical placement was asserted.
- Regional vegetation is an art proposal using recognizable plant families, not ecological certification. Saguaros are restricted to Yard Commons; river groves, cultivated rows, mountain pines and dry dune-margin plants remain distinct.
- References in assets.json are text direction/source roles. No image was submitted as a visual input; all six calls were brand-new built-in generations with the exact lock embedded.
