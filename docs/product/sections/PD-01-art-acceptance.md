## Contract

### What this brief is

This brief is the acceptance instrument for production art. It states what a reviewer observes, what evidence a physical claim requires, which views and provenance fields a submission carries, and which values are not selected. It is not a commission: no row here names an asset to produce, a batch to fund, a dimension to build or a motion to animate, and no row authorizes image generation. Under HF-01 line 74 a concept image remains a reference until a product or content card admits it, and under BIBLE line 689 concept art is never presented as proof of implementation.

Three separations hold throughout. An **acceptance check** (AC) is something a reviewer can see in the submitted image or model. A **required-evidence row** (PE) is a physical property that no image can settle, with the evidence class that can. An **unresolved question** is a product value Andrew owns; it appears as a question ID and never as a number, a name or a selection.
