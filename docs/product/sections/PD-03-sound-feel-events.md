## Contract

### What this brief fixes

This brief is the sound and feel event contract that TH-17 reads. It fixes twenty-two event rows mapping each named family to a proposed physical source, a trigger meaning, the Bible pages that meaning is checked against and the committed moment the admitted sibling already names for it; seven browser-proof rows stating what the two synthesized proofs actually play; fourteen accepted invariants; fourteen unselected artistic details; fourteen unset parameters; six audition cells and two fatigue methods; and five ordered walks.

Every cue row carries exactly one label. **proposal** is a physical source or trigger treatment from Bible Section 8's creative proposal (BIBLE lines 747, 769, 784) that no one has auditioned. **browser proof** is what the synthesized WebAudio code in `src/` plays at this base; it is a starting vocabulary and never a production cue (BIBLE line 747). **accepted invariant** is a rule already carried by the approved Section 6 pages, by Section 8's threats-first priority, by an admitted technical section or by a recorded answer of Andrew's; each such row names that carrier.

It decides no bus, mixer, voice pool or engine audio API, no cue ownership rule, no voice lifetime and no pause or scene behaviour of audio; those are TH-17's under the seam below.

### Event map

`Committed moment` names the HF-04a phase or callback class and, where one runs, the HF-07b transition that the admitted sibling already names for the same moment. A cue sounds from that published value and never earlier.

**UI-only input exception.** Andrew decided at TRACKING EV-0453 that menu and interface navigation sounds play at the input moment. The exception is limited to UI feedback and is the only departure from the rule above: it covers SE-14 and no other row. The click confirms that an input was received; it does not say that a request was admitted, it reaches no `submit_action`, and it marks nothing applied or acknowledged. Every gameplay cue, including attacks, damage, pickups, danger, revival and defeat, still waits for its published value. The exception departs from three admitted TH-16 rules: TH-16 RI-14 line 181 plays a cue only from a published fact, in F-PROCESS or F-SIGNAL for an engine completion; TH-16 RV-5 line 55 derives feedback playing from one published fact that `present_committed_result` or `present_snapshot` delivered; and TH-16 line 304, Seam 4, states that an audible cue consumes a published value. An input-moment click has no published fact and runs outside F-PROCESS, so the departure is recorded as specification amendment request AM-H8-006 (`artifacts/handoff-8-2026-09-16/amendments/AM-H8-006/request.md`), accepted as Andrew's product direction and not admitted. **Gate:** until AM-H8-006 is admitted by a TH-16 revision, SE-14 sounds from its published value under the rule above like every other cue, and TH-17 may not realize input-moment playback.
