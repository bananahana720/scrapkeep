# Decision register

5 September 2026. This is the one ordered list of decisions that await Andrew after the [design audit](../DESIGN-AUDIT.md) and the five-lane deep-research pass ([review notes](../research-2026-09-05/REVIEW-NOTES.md)). It exists so the next session can open with a bounded decision conversation before any implementation. Each entry names the question, the options the desk considered, the recommendation the desk made and where it made it, what the ruling unblocks, and the default that applies while the entry stays open. A ruling is recorded in [decisions.tsv](../decisions.tsv) and propagated by the owning document, per [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). An unruled entry keeps its stated default. A default is the desk's recommendation labeled as such, not a ruling made on Andrew's behalf. Where two documents disagree, the entry says so. No entry invents a recommendation; an entry with none says "no recommendation in the desk".

Two backlog items are evidence gates rather than decisions and are not listed below. [P01](BACKLOG.md) (Andrew plays the orchard) and [P02](BACKLOG.md) (ranged circling and pacing after P01) produce the playtest evidence that several Tier A and Tier B rulings should wait for. The register notes where a ruling depends on them.

Tiering. Tier A gates the next build increment, the audit's increment 1 feel experiment, and the T01/T02 tooling choices. Tier B gates a later increment: enemy grammar (increment 2), systems spine (increment 3), expedition and Scrapbook (increment 4), or the parallel art and writing track. Tier C can run on its stated default and is revisited on a named trigger.
