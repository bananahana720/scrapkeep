# Close the selected sprint

Read [the operating manual's Sprint closure procedure](../../../ORCHESTRATOR.md#sprint-closure), [TRACKING](../../../TRACKING.md), the selected [BACKLOG](../../../BACKLOG.md) cards and their exact source contracts. Follow the procedure in order. Do not infer authorization from ready tasks.

Stop dispatch and reconcile leases. Reconcile accepted amendments and their propagation before pinning the integrated commit. Run the required integrated checks on that identity. Retain evidence and classify closure as passed, failed or pending_evidence. Do not replace missing human feedback with automated play or treat a technical proof as a Pillars pass.

Review rejected work and blockers, capture causes and create or revise atomic cards. Reuse existing IDs on repeated closeout. Prepare one next-sprint proposal of at most four S/M cards with an input-ready initial dispatch set. Keep it awaiting_review. Record the receipt and append the result in TRACKING. Never start the proposed sprint.

Report the checked commit, required gate outcomes, evidence limits, unresolved amendments, retro actions and the next approval-ready scope. Say SPRINT COMPLETE only when the required integrated gate and closure checks pass.
