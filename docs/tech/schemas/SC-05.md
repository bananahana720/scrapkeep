## Contract

### What this contract covers

This contract fixes five definition types, T1 to T5. It fixes their allocation to HF-06a rows, twenty-four diagnostics, a save treatment for every type and row, eight paper round-trip expectations, six executable qualification obligations and thirty-two parameter rows. Every parameter row carries a source line or a question ID. The Bible's areas are the region type, not a sixth type. Events and secrets are withheld. The contract fixes no number that no cited source states.

### Area is the region

The Bible uses "area" for the same thing as a region. Line 169 calls the five area pages "possible worlds", line 578 calls a world a run-region and the illustrated areas its comparison catalogue, and HF-06a N-015 at line 88 calls a region-theme reference the rule that "themed drops belong to their area". A separate area type would be a second editable truth for one noun (TADR-003 line 35). The card's "areas" are therefore covered by T1. The Bible's "area milestones" name a progression allocation, not a definition; they are TH-07's and stay under Q-P10.
