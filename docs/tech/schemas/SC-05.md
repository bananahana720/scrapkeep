## Contract

### What this contract covers

This contract fixes five definition types, T1 to T5. It fixes their allocation to HF-06a rows, twenty-four diagnostics, a save treatment for every type and row, eight paper round-trip expectations, six executable qualification obligations and thirty-two parameter rows. Every parameter row carries a source line or a question ID. The Bible's areas are the region type, not a sixth type. Events and secret rooms reach a level through SC-04's objective definitions (FR-03), not through a type here. The contract fixes no number that no cited source states.

### Area is the region

The Bible uses "area" for the same thing as a region. Line 169 calls the five area pages "possible worlds", line 578 calls a world a run-region and the illustrated areas its comparison catalogue, and HF-06a N-015 at line 88 calls a region-theme reference the rule that "themed drops belong to their area". A separate area type would be a second editable truth for one noun (TADR-003 line 35). The card's "areas" are therefore covered by T1. The Bible's "area milestones" name a progression allocation, not a definition; they are TH-07's and stay under Q-P10.

### Events and secret rooms are F-04 objectives

Card revision 2 excludes events and secrets from this family. AM-H8-004, accepted as option 1, settles who owns them: event and secret-room types are F-04 objective definitions owned by SC-04. TH-06 UN-05 at line 119 already placed them in "the level's objective definitions". SC-04 defines them as T7 ObjectiveDefinition, whose `kind` vocabulary is ST-22 under Q-P38. No field in T1 to T5 names an objective kind, and none is needed.

A level reaches an event or secret room by one path, FR-03:

1. The level's modules are the T2 records its T5 constraint selects. A boss level's exit also names `boss_encounter_id`.
2. Each T2 `encounter_slots[].encounter_id`, and a boss exit's `boss_encounter_id`, resolves to an SC-04 T5 EncounterDefinition (FR-01, FR-02).
3. That encounter's `objective_ids` resolves to one or more SC-04 T7 ObjectiveDefinition records (SC-04 line 277, nonempty by D5-01 at line 290, reference SR-09 at line 409). An event or a secret room is such an objective.

Nothing in this contract references T7 directly. A slot that names a T7 record is a wrong-family reference (DG-13), because a slot names an encounter and an objective reaches the level only through that encounter. Which objective kinds exist, and their completion, failure, reward and reconfiguration boundaries, stay Q-P38's. Under AM-H8-004 option 1 a respec opportunity is an objective or reward boundary on this path, and its rules stay Q-P18's. BIBLE line 558's awards from events and secret rooms stay with TH-09 LA-01 at line 61.
