## Contract

### Scope

This contract covers devices, companion chassis, interchangeable companion components and command capabilities, and nothing else. It fixes four definition types. A deployed device, an assembled companion and an acting companion are not definitions; TH-10 lines 44 to 49 place them in `EncounterState` and `RunState`, and this contract adds no field to either. Attacks, items, abilities, glossary entries and presentation assets are foreign and are reached only by the references named below.
