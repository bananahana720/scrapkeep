## Scenarios

1. A contributor copies H-lightning's frame statistic into a native limit. Reject the promotion: historical browser classification remains, and N-frame stays unmeasured until Q-E03 admits a native measurement and Andrew rules on the budget.
2. A proposed frame, memory, load or asset value lacks a unit or the `proposed:Q-E03` tag. Reject the row. A measured value instead needs a `measured:<artifact path>` tag with retained evidence; neither tag alone supplies missing workload, target, method or owner fields.
3. A row is labelled both approved and historical. Reject it and separate the approved rule from the supporting historical observation. Exactly one classification per row is required even when multiple sources apply.
4. S0-01's successful headless boot is offered as performance evidence. Retain the boot result and keep N-frame through N-assets unmeasured. The re-verification trigger has fired, but the receipt contains no representative gameplay budget measurement.
5. The operational export succeeds on Linux and generates a Windows executable. Retain the synthetic export/Linux execution scope; do not close Windows execution, clean-machine qualification, controller feel or Q-E04.
6. A save optimization meets a proposed latency number but acknowledges before qualified commit, or resumes an ambiguous older living save. Reject it under C-save, regardless of speed. Performance cannot weaken durable lifecycle authority.
7. A proposed cheaper renderer hides committed threat geometry or normally grown mechs become trapped. Reject that candidate against C-geometry/C-movement and return the proposal for revision. Q-E03/Q-E04 do not authorize weaker product invariants.
8. A new Q-E03 measurement names complete target/workload/protocol/owner fields. Retain its exact artifact and source version, revise the corresponding unmeasured row for its measured scope, and request Andrew's budget ruling. Other targets and workloads remain unqualified.

## Unresolved questions

[Q-E03](../../../BACKLOG.md#q-e03) is open. Andrew owns representative Windows/Linux hardware, distributions/drivers, controller devices and budget rulings, with a Verification measurement proposal. Closing evidence must name targets, workloads, measurement protocol and budget decisions. D28 explicitly forbids adopting a budget before naming the target machine. This document supplies an admission skeleton and chooses no hardware or numerical limits.

[Q-E04](../../../BACKLOG.md#q-e04) is open. Runtime/Rendering recommends a renderer/backend; Andrew decides qualification and fallback scope. Closing evidence must record that choice with evidence requirements against approved art and named hardware. No engine or language reevaluation follows from this question.

Neither question blocks completion of this register. Both gate dependent qualification and implementation choices. Open gameplay tuning, capacities and content questions remain with their Bible systems and backlog owners; this register creates no answers or launch catalogue.

## Evidence limits

This register defines documentation requirements and traces historical evidence. It establishes no new engine behavior, performance ceiling, hardware support, physical controller feel or human enjoyment result. Historical yard summaries lack a verified capture commit and raw samples. Lightning retains stronger capture identity and frame samples but covers one browser/machine/fixture. Native synthetic boot, exports, saves and UI establish their recorded technical proof only. No build, native/browser run, CI dispatch, dependency change or game feature belongs to HF-02.
