### Provisional preview and its honesty rule

1. **Provisional means never promoted.** An `AimPreview` is a published display value. It is not a commitment, it is not admissible as the commitment's geometry, and the commitment recomputes its descriptor against the post-movement world at P4 rather than reusing the preview's (TADR-002 line 30; HF-04a line 65).
2. **Weapon-origin obstruction.** The preview's obstruction test runs from `origin`, not from the camera. A camera can see around an obstruction the weapon cannot fire through; the preview must therefore report `validity` as blocked in exactly that case (BIBLE line 493; ADR 0003 line 11).
3. **Honest display.** A blocked or invalid action must be displayed as blocked or invalid. Decoration never grants a hit the resolved geometry does not support, and a drawing cannot independently choose its impact point (BIBLE line 493; ADR 0003 line 11).
4. **One authoritative definition.** Preview, telegraph, damage and effects consume the same descriptor family. Preserving one authoritative geometry definition when extending it is the browser-proof precedent (ADR 0003 line 13) and the production rule (TADR-002 line 30; BIBLE lines 493, 606).
5. **Cost of refusal.** If the provisional query cannot be completed, the preview shows blocked or invalid rather than a valid-looking shape; it never falls back to an unqueried drawing (BIBLE line 493; HF-04a line 71).

Six of HF-03a's seven modules appear above: Session, Encounter, CollisionQueries, ContentAdmission, InputAdapter and Presentation, plus the `Values` sink. **Persistence appears nowhere and edges D04 and D09 are unused**, because nothing an attack produces is durable at this base: the committed descriptor lives in `AttackLedger` and ends at `retire` (TH-01 line 60), and the only durable consequence of a fight is the encounter result Session accepts and TH-01 routes (AT-31). This section invokes no command outside HF-03a's exposed-command lists, travels on no pair outside HF-03b's adjacency list, and adds nothing to HF-03b's unlisted-edge register: DF-01 aim authority, DF-02 in-encounter definition resolution, DF-03 presentation asset resolution and DF-04 input sampling all apply unchanged at lines 91 to 94.

### Owned data

An attack reads and writes exactly the records below, through the interfaces above, and nothing else.

1. **Encounter writes `AttackLedger`, and no other module writes it.** It holds the attack commitments, declared windups, resolved geometry, targets, random outcomes and applied-result identities (HF-03a line 46). It also writes `EncounterState` for the committed actor pose, health and prepared actions, `EncounterRandomState` for the single draw, `TickState` for the phase position and `EncounterInbox` for submitted commands. It holds no writable `RunState` alias and resolves no definition ID itself (HF-03a line 46; HF-03b line 92).
2. **CollisionQueries writes `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState`.** It answers `query_attack`, `resolve_movement` and `interpret_contacts` on Encounter's ordered request and publishes no damage, reward or progression mutation; Encounter alone commits the authoritative gameplay pose, and Presentation's interpolated transform never feeds this world (HF-03a lines 33, 47).
3. **Session writes `SessionControl`, and writes `RunState` or `ProfileState` only when it accepts an encounter result.** No individual attack settles anything in the run (HF-03a line 57; TH-01 line 91).
4. **Presentation writes `PresentationState` and `FeedbackState` from published values.** Cue consumption settles no reward and marks no attack applied; a view chooses no target, marks nothing applied and rerolls no miscast (HF-03a line 51; HF-03b line 79; TADR-002 line 30).
5. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`.** An input callback submits a request; no cast, damage, cooldown or encounter progress follows directly from it, and clearing physical input cannot mutate a prepared action (HF-03a lines 36, 50; HF-04a line 46).
6. **ContentAdmission writes `CatalogueAdmissionState` and `CatalogueRegistry`.** An attack definition is an immutable admitted value delivered through Session; a handle that resolves through ContentAdmission on access is a hidden edge and a defect (HF-03a line 48; HF-03b line 68).
7. **Persistence writes nothing for an attack.** No attack fact is staged, committed or acknowledged at this base.

One attack, from the first prepare to the published impact, runs in this order: an input edge is sampled and read as an `ActionRequest`; Session routes it with `submit_action`; the next eligible C-TICK admits it at P1 with one `read_frame`; P2 integrates the aim into the authoritative pose and builds the provisional descriptor; P3 resolves movers and identifies the post-movement world; P4 issues the provisional `query_attack` and marks `validity`; P6 publishes `AimPreview`; Session reads it on D01 and presents it on D06. On a later tick P1 captures the release edge, P3 resolves movers again, P4 revalidates the prepared action and resolves geometry, targets and the random outcome once into `AttackLedger`, P5 applies that outcome once, P6 publishes `CommittedAttack`, and Session reads it on D01 and presents it on D06. An enemy's attack uses the same order with its declaration at P2 of the preparing tick and its single target resolution at P4 of the executing tick.
