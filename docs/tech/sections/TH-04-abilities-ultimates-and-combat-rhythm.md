### Action availability

### Owned data

An ability reads and writes exactly the records below, through the interfaces above, and nothing else.

1. **Encounter writes `EncounterState`, and no other module writes it.** It holds the availability inputs the encounter owns: the actor's resources and cooldowns, the accrued charge, the active devices and companion action state, the open effect windows and the prepared actions (HF-03a line 46). It also writes `AttackLedger` for an activation's commitment, geometry, targets, outcome and applied-result identity, `EncounterRandomState` for the single draw, `TickState` for the phase position and `EncounterInbox` for submitted commands. It holds no writable `RunState` alias and resolves no definition ID itself (HF-03a line 46; HF-03b line 92).
2. **Session writes `SessionControl`, `RunState`, `ProfileState`, `EffectiveBuild` and `PendingTransition`.** It verifies the opportunity for an equipment change, refuses a respec without an authorized one, settles an accepted result once — including a slot unlock — and rebuilds `EffectiveBuild` from all current selections so a removed selection stops contributing (HF-03a lines 45, 57; HF-05 line 56; TADR-002 line 32). No individual activation settles anything in the run.
3. **CollisionQueries writes `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState`.** It answers `resolve_movement`, `interpret_contacts` and `query_attack` on Encounter's ordered request and publishes no damage, reward or progression mutation; Encounter alone commits the authoritative gameplay pose (HF-03a lines 33, 47).
4. **ContentAdmission writes `CatalogueAdmissionState` and `CatalogueRegistry`.** A slot, node, ability, ultimate or device definition is an immutable admitted value delivered through Session, with no accumulating per-run field; a handle that resolves through ContentAdmission on access is a hidden edge and a defect (HF-03a line 48; HF-05 lines 35 to 37; HF-03b line 68).
5. **Presentation writes `PresentationState` and `FeedbackState` from published values.** It displays available actions, cooldowns and ultimate charge and distinguishes the dashboard's four slot states from the published selection and the current opportunity; cue consumption settles no reward, marks no action applied, decrements no cooldown and accrues no charge (HF-03a lines 37, 51; HF-07a line 128).
6. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`.** An input callback submits a request; no cast, cooldown, charge or encounter progress follows directly from it, and clearing physical input cannot mutate a prepared action (HF-03a lines 36, 50; HF-04a line 46).
7. **Persistence writes nothing for an ability.** No cooldown, charge, effect window or activation count is staged, committed or acknowledged. The only durable trace an ability's use leaves is the milestone a settled accomplishment writes into `run.checkpoint.build.slots[].unlocked`, `.tree_id` and `.node_keys`, which is TH-05's and HF-07b's to commit and is named here only as the fact AA-02 and AA-03 read (HF-07a lines 126 to 130; TH-05 line 161).

### Seams stated and not decided

**Seam 1: the committed attack, to TH-03.** An activation that produces an attack is a TH-03 commitment without exception: the nine descriptor parts, the provisional preview and its honesty rule, the weapon-origin obstruction, the height band, the enemy's four phases and the chaos rules are all TH-03's and are restated nowhere here. This section adds no descriptor part, no query and no presentation record. It fixes only what surrounds the commitment: whether the action was available, what its commitment costs in cooldown and charge, and how long what it opened lasts.

**Seam 2: the slot unlock and its durability, to TH-01, TH-05 and HF-07b.** An accomplishment that unlocks a slot or a node leaves the encounter as an `EncounterResult`, is settled once by Session at RL3 and is committed by Persistence through HF-07b's four stages into the three `run.checkpoint.build.slots[]` fields TH-05 line 161 names. This section names those fields as the availability facts AA-02 and AA-03 read and names no Persistence command, no transition and no commit boundary.

**Seam 3: the kit itself, to Q-P11, Q-P13 and Q-P20.** Which actions a character has, which slot each occupies, what each does, which of them charge the ultimate, how much they charge, at what threshold it activates, how long anything lasts and what any node changes are content and tuning inputs. Every such value is a row in the parameter table under its question ID, and the contract above is written so that answering them adds no interface, no record, no event and no ordering rule.
