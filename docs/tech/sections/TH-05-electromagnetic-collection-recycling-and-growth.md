### Owned data

This contract reads and writes exactly the records the carried-state table assigns, through the interfaces above, and nothing else.

1. **Encounter writes `EncounterState`, `TickState`, `AttackLedger`, `EncounterRandomState` and `EncounterInbox` and no other record.** The collection controller is a composed controller inside Encounter's boundary, not another mutation owner (HF-03a line 21). It owns each fragment's identity, committed pose, attraction intent and consumed flag, and the encounter's accomplishment facts. It receives the definitions, the capability set and a seed as values and holds no writable `RunState` alias (HF-03a line 46). Its records end at `retire`.
2. **CollisionQueries writes `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState`.** It drives and interprets physical-body updates for the actor and for every attracted fragment, owns query ordering and the identified post-movement world, and returns resolutions, contact interpretations and query results. It commits no pose, applies no intake and awards nothing (HF-03a lines 33, 47, 61).
3. **Session writes `SessionControl`, `RunState`, `ProfileState`, `EffectiveBuild` and `PendingTransition`.** It settles each accepted result once, rebuilds the capability set from all current selections so a removed part stops contributing and repeated swaps accumulate nothing, and installs the result as values (HF-03a line 57; TADR-002 line 32). It never writes a durable record.
4. **Persistence writes `SaveTransactionState`, `DurableAggregate`, `PreferencesRecord` and `RecoveryState`, and it alone stages or replaces them** (HF-03a line 49). Session requests the transition on D04 and supplies the accepted inputs; Persistence builds, validates, commits and acknowledges the candidate (HF-07b lines 37 to 40). HF-10 required fix RF-6 at line 64 records the one admitted scenario step that reads otherwise; this section states the ownership HF-03a fixes.
5. **ContentAdmission writes `CatalogueAdmissionState` and `CatalogueRegistry`.** The run's pin selects a revision without modifying the catalogue, and a fabrication never edits an admitted definition: a construction changes selections and the rebuilt capability set, never a `DefinitionView` (HF-03a line 48; HF-05 lines 35 to 37).
6. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`.** Clearing physical input cannot mutate a prepared action, a pause state, a save or the run, and an input callback only submits a request (HF-03a line 50; HF-04a line 46).
7. **Presentation writes `PresentationState` and `FeedbackState` from published values.** The intake cue, the accumulated silhouette, the fabrication animation and every impact response are display state. Presentation performs no physics query, chooses no target, marks nothing collected and rerolls nothing (HF-03a lines 37, 51; HF-03b line 79).

### Physical growth constraints

TH-02 bounds growth from outside and hands the inside to this card (TH-02 lines 174, 248). The boundary conditions it fixes are adopted unchanged: the extent is a value, it changes only at a committed tick and control boundary, it is resolved through CollisionQueries, it carries no handling penalty and no mass cost, and it must be admitted by ordinary routes. What this section adds is the mechanism by which any Q-P10 and Q-P22 answer can be expressed without a new module, command, record or edge, and the four constraints every answer must satisfy.

1. **The only route into a changed extent is a rebuild.** Session changes a selection, rebuilds `EffectiveBuild` from all current selections and installs the result with `install_effective_build` at a Session-authorized boundary (CI-20). There is no second route: Encounter cannot grow itself, CollisionQueries cannot resize a body on its own authority, and Presentation cannot change an extent it only displays.
2. **That boundary is the intermission, and not a live encounter.** TADR-002 line 32 places equipment changes between fights, TH-01's RL4 is the admitted intermission, and HF-03a line 32 leaves `install_effective_build` at "a Session-authorized boundary" without enumerating which boundaries are authorized. Q-P10's recorded partial answer of 16 September 2026 (TRACKING EV-0255) settles the reading for this contract: a milestone upgrade earned mid-encounter grows the chassis and becomes usable at the next intermission only, so CI-20 installs at RL4 and there is no in-encounter install. CP-23 and CP-24 carry the rule; how much visible mass one milestone adds stays open at CP-19.
3. **Growth adds presence and takes nothing away.** No mandatory handling penalty, no steering-authority reduction and no body-mass cost for a routine action (BIBLE line 481), and no required scrap shedding, which is the mandatory mass sacrifice BIBLE line 513 refuses (CO-12). The practical route clearance, the protected cockpit and the recognizable working equipment survive the clutter (BIBLE line 511).
4. **The world is the arbiter of what fits.** At the installed extent, movement resolves against the physical world; the actor passes or does not. The runtime never shrinks the actor, never grants a pass-through and never spends mass to squeeze through. Level design must test the positional movement loop at ordinary maximum growth, and a passage that traps an ordinarily developed chassis is reported as a content defect against BIBLE line 487 (TH-02 lines 129, 189).

The ordinary maximum envelope and the minimum clearance a route must therefore admit stay exactly where TH-02 left them, unset under Q-P22 at its rows MP-16 and MP-17; this section restates them at CP-21 and CP-22 and chooses neither. TH-03's TP-20 leaves the same envelope's effect on weapon-origin height and reach unset, and nothing here narrows it.
