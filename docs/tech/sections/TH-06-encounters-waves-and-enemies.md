1. **A wave boundary is not a level boundary.** A cleared wave advances nothing durable: no transition stages, `run.checkpoint.next_entrance` does not move, no reward settles and no build installs. 2. **A boss is a world fact, not a level fact.** BIBLE line 519 gives each world a boss and states that an ordinary level does not need one; docs/DISCOVERY.md line 13 records that "boss at every level" is superseded. No rule here places a boss in an ordinary level, and no rule here forbids one either: which levels carry a miniboss or a boss is [Q-P23](../../../BACKLOG.md#q-p23)'s and [Q-P25](../../../BACKLOG.md#q-p25)'s.
3. **A completed level is not a cleared population.** BIBLE line 519 makes a level an accomplishment and permits exploration and events inside it, so "every enemy is dead" is one possible completion boundary and not the contract. Which boundary applies is [Q-P38](../../../BACKLOG.md#q-p38)'s, and rule EO-06 below fixes only that completion is read from objective state and published at P6.
4. **Nothing a wave produces survives the level.** `EncounterState` ends at `retire` (TH-01 line 60; HF-03a line 46), so an interrupted level loses its waves entirely and resumes at the banked entrance (TH-11 line 229; HF-07b line 147).

### Owned data

The schedule reads and writes exactly the records the carried-state table assigns, through the interfaces above, and nothing else.

1. **Encounter writes its five records and no other.** Wave and objective state, arrivals, actor health and poses, resources and cooldowns live in `EncounterState`; commitments and declared windups live in `AttackLedger`; the tick position lives in `TickState`; the single draw comes from `EncounterRandomState`; submitted commands wait in `EncounterInbox`. It holds no writable `RunState` alias and resolves no definition ID itself (HF-03a line 46; HF-03b line 92).
2. **An enemy, a wave scheduler and an objective evaluator are composed controllers inside that boundary, not owners.** None of them holds a record of its own, admits its own attack or advances a tick; a future field belonging to any of them enters `EncounterState` or `AttackLedger` by contract revision (HF-03a lines 21, 55).
3. **CollisionQueries writes `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState`** and answers `attach_world`, `resolve_movement`, `query_attack`, `interpret_contacts` and `detach_world` on Encounter's ordered request. It publishes no damage, reward or progression mutation, and Presentation's interpolated transform never feeds its world (HF-03a lines 33, 47).
4. **ContentAdmission writes `CatalogueAdmissionState` and `CatalogueRegistry`.** An enemy, wave or objective definition is an immutable admitted value delivered through Session; a replacement candidate never edits a cached Resource a live encounter still references, and admission happens at a safe boundary outside a live encounter (HF-03a line 48; HF-05 lines 35, 64).
5. **Session writes `SessionControl`, `RunState`, `ProfileState`, `EffectiveBuild` and `PendingTransition`,** and writes to the run only when it accepts an encounter result. No individual wave, arrival or commitment settles anything in the run (HF-03a line 57; TH-01 line 91).
6. **Persistence alone stages, validates, commits and acknowledges the completed level's candidate and writes its file** (HF-03a line 49; HF-07b lines 37 to 40). Session supplies the accepted inputs on D04 and waits. HF-10 required fix RF-6 at line 64 recorded the one admitted scenario step that read otherwise; FR-01 applied it.
7. **Presentation writes `PresentationState` and `FeedbackState` from published values.** A threat cue, an arrival marker and an objective readout are displays of committed facts; cue consumption settles no reward, marks no attack applied and admits no arrival (HF-03a line 51; HF-03b line 79).
8. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`** and holds nothing about the schedule. Clearing physical input cannot mutate a prepared action, a wave or an objective (HF-03a lines 36, 50).
