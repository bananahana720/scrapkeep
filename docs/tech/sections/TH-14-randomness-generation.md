## Contract

### What this section fixes

It fixes four random responsibilities, each with one owner, one stream, one consumption position and one saved form; ten independence rules; seven stream-identity and durability rules; a seven-field level-generation request and a four-part result with nine generation rules; eight clearance and validation rules; six stored-outcome-against-seed rules; seven repeatability rules across engine, OS and timing; twenty-six interface rows, each naming one HF-03a command, published result or owned record on one HF-03b edge; seven owned-data rules; fourteen ordering rules; twelve edge cases; nine failure modes; and twenty-six tunable parameters, each carrying either a source line or a question ID. It fixes no number that no cited source states.

It is not a runtime. No stream named here has been consumed, no level named here has been generated, no candidate has been rejected and no engine, browser or build ran for it.

### Where this section sits

Four admitted contracts already fix one responsibility each and each of them stops at the same place. TH-11 RS-01 to RS-05 at its lines 77 to 81 fix that an offer's presented options are the stored options, restored by value and never regenerated. TH-06 line 41 fixes that a level's whole durable trace is the advance of `run.checkpoint.next_entrance` and its scope's `run.checkpoint.generation_decisions`. TH-09 LA-03 and LU-02 at its lines 63 and 177 fix that an award's outcome is drawn at most once inside the tick that committed it and is then a stored fact. TH-13 XS-01 to XS-07 at its lines 122 to 128 fix that the miscast draw comes from `EncounterRandomState`, that the encounter never reads or advances a run-level stream, and that the only durable generator fields are the two `rng_state` groups.

None of them says which stream each responsibility consumes, whether two of them share one, what a level-generation request may read, what makes a candidate level admissible, or what repeatability actually holds. **This section specifies exactly those five things and adds no sixth.** TH-13 Seam 2 at its line 281 hands the first, the third through the `stream_id` vocabulary, and the fifth here in terms; blueprint line 81 hands the choice between separate seeded streams and explicit stored outcomes to scoping, and the answer below is **both, applied to different things**: separate streams carry future generation, and explicit stored outcomes carry everything already presented or resolved.

### Owned data

The four responsibilities read and write exactly the records below, through the interfaces above, and nothing else.

1. **Session writes `RunState` and owns the route and layout streams inside it**, together with the accepted routes, generation decisions, resolved rewards and offers those streams produced. It settles each accepted reward, offer and route result once, and it writes no durable record itself (HF-03a lines 45, 57). It also writes `SessionControl`, `ProfileState`, `EffectiveBuild` and `PendingTransition`.
2. **Encounter writes `EncounterRandomState` and owns the loot and miscast streams inside it**, consuming the miscast stream at P4 and the loot stream at P5. It also writes `AttackLedger` for the commitment and its drawn outcome, `EncounterState`, `TickState` and `EncounterInbox`. It holds no writable `RunState` alias, resolves no definition ID and generates no level (HF-03a line 46; HF-03b line 92).
3. **CollisionQueries writes `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState`.** It answers `attach_world`, `resolve_movement`, `query_attack`, `interpret_contacts` and `detach_world` on Encounter's ordered request, holds no generator, decides no outcome and assembles no level (HF-03a lines 33, 47).
4. **ContentAdmission writes `CatalogueAdmissionState` and `CatalogueRegistry`.** Generation policies, region, entrance and item definitions are immutable admitted values delivered through Session with no accumulating per-run field, and an unknown required rule blocks admission (HF-03a line 48; HF-05 lines 35 to 37, 51).
5. **Persistence writes `SaveTransactionState`, `DurableAggregate`, `PreferencesRecord` and `RecoveryState`.** It alone stages, validates, commits and acknowledges every candidate carrying an offer, a route step, a decision set, a reward or a stream entry, and it never awards anything or edits Session memory (HF-03a line 49; HF-07b lines 37 to 40).
6. **Presentation writes `PresentationState` and `FeedbackState` from published values.** It holds no gameplay generator, rerolls no loot, miscast or route offer, chooses no destination and records only a `UiRequest` value Session reads (HF-03a line 51; HF-03b line 79).
7. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`.** Input frames are transient and are never restored from a save, and no draw follows directly from an input callback (HF-03a lines 36, 50; HF-04a line 46).
