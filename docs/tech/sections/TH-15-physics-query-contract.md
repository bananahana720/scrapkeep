## Contract

### What this section fixes

It fixes six positions of the collision world across an encounter's life; twenty-two interface rows, each naming one HF-03a command, published result or owned record on one HF-03b edge; the complete per-phase query order of one tick with the two phases that may query and the phases and callback families that may not; the synchronized collision-world snapshot, its revision identity, what may change it and what it is not; seven named coordinate conversions with their direction, owner and prohibition; nine contact-interpretation rules; sixteen ordering rules; thirteen controlled engine fixtures, each with a setup, inputs, a query sequence, an expected observation and the TH-02 and TH-03 rules the observation confirms; a review of every fixture's expected observation against both the movement and the attack page; a browser-prototype separation table; twelve edge cases; nine failure modes; and twenty-five tunable parameters, each carrying either a source line or a question ID. It fixes no number that no cited source states.

It is not a runtime, and no fixture below is a test. **No trial named here has been executed.** No engine world has been attached, no query has been issued, no contact has been interpreted, no coordinate has been converted, and no engine, browser or build ran for this section. No engine behaviour is established by any of it.

Two modifiers apply across positions rather than being positions. **Paused** is O3: C-TICK admission and gameplay physics freeze, the world stands exactly where the last complete tick left it, and Presentation snaps or holds to the last committed state rather than visually advancing it (HF-04a lines 79, 89). **Retired** is `retire`: the world is detached, and no physics or contact result from it may enter another encounter (HF-04a line 109; HF-03b line 179).

Nothing in any position is durable. `CollisionWorldState` and `QueryBatchState` have no durable projection, are discarded with the world and do not survive encounter replacement (TH-01 line 61; HF-03a line 47). No pose, body transform, world revision, contact, query result, collision layer or physical extent appears in the closed seventy-field save surface (HF-07a lines 223, 295), and an implementation may not add one.

### Owned data

4. **InputAdapter writes `PhysicalInputState` and `InputContextState`.** An input callback only submits a request; clearing physical input mutates no prepared action, pose or world (HF-03a lines 36, 50; HF-04a line 46).
