On 2026-10-10 Andrew asked to "decompile" Risk of Rain 2, Gunfire Reborn, Roboquest and Isaac for Scrapkeep, then set the line: no copyright infringement, borrow systems and structure, not assets. The result is card CS-01 and the pack at `sketches/core-loop/design-v3/research/comps/` (commit c8e1427, accepted at 6939b93 on `claude/steam-game-asset-decompile-907d39`): a 17-row borrow list, a reconciled pocket-placement spec, 11 Risk of Rain 2 notes, 5 Isaac notes. CS-02 (Gunfire Reborn) started the same day on Andrew's "move on to Gunfire Reborn"; CS-03 (Roboquest) is defined, not started.

**Decisions Andrew made that session:** stages are one open stage with authored pockets; great rooms are layouts you can solve, enemy mixes that force movement, optional risk pockets; companions are a crew of scrap bots that grow, persist and have personality, with no direct command controls. The crew replaced the Bible's one main companion on 2026-10-10: `BIBLE.md` § 6 amended in place (heading kept for anchors), `adr/TADR-011-scrap-bot-crew.md`, TRACKING EV-0599, commit 69ca515. Cap (proposed three), roles and prices stay open under Q-P15; TH-10, HF-07a, SC-06, PD-03 and TH-12 rows are listed as superseded in `docs/tech/sections/README.md` and wait for a heavy-lane revision card.

**Tooling that worked, kept in `~/scratch/game-study/tools/`:** ILSpy 10.1.1 runs on the installed .NET 10 runtime without the SDK (`dotnet tools/ilspycmd/pkg/tools/net10.0/any/ilspycmd.dll`, fetched from NuGet; pacman needed sudo, which prompted); UnityPy 1.25.4 plus TypeTreeGeneratorAPI in a uv project reads Unity 2021 bundles and MonoBehaviour fields (`measure.py`, `budgets.py`, `stage_graphs.py`); Isaac ships its own Linux `tools/ResourceExtractor/resource_extractor <game> <out>`, and `isaac/stb.py` parses the room files (STB1 magic, width and height before shape, spawns before doors). Gunfire Reborn and Roboquest are IL2CPP (`GameAssembly.dll`), so their logic is not decompiled under the cards; the wiki and play notes are primary and the asset tables are read for budgets only. The decompiled tree, measurement CSVs and extracted Isaac data were deleted after acceptance; scout reports stay under `~/scratch/game-study/*/reports/`.

**CS-02 Gunfire Reborn (accepted 2026-10-11, EV-0600):** Andrew authorized "make a clone, not mess with the actual Steam files, and go deep" with Haiku probes. What opened on a Unity 2018 IL2CPP game: the asset bundles are standard UnityFS behind a 50-byte prefix (`measure_prefixed.py`, 3,478 bundles, zero errors); 234 gameplay tables ship as gzip JSON TextAssets keyed by id (`probe/gf_*.py`), including the stage slot-and-pool table but no combat stats or spawn waves; the `mdata*.fls` stage chunks are a `0SLF` header plus a chain of u32-length protobuf frames from offset 18 (`probe/fls_footprints.py`), 118 of 296 plain, with floor bounding boxes and a 2-unit walkability raster; chunk ids join to no table. What stayed shut and is not worth the day: encrypted IL2CPP metadata (Il2CppDumper "metadata not found or encrypted"; it also needs `</dev/null` or it core-dumps on "press any key"), the packed native logic library, 148 opaque chunks, the `FLS\0` archives. The wiki's API gives wikitext and rendered HTML for the table-heavy pages. Review lessons repeated from CS-01: writers reproduce table identifiers verbatim unless told to paraphrase, and a lead note will silently replace Andrew's own category set with the reference game's; brief both bans up front. 10 notes plus `maps-compared.md` started; 26 borrow rows total.

**CS-03 Roboquest (accepted 2026-10-11, fa0810c):** Unreal 4.26, one IoStore container, fully readable with retoc built from source (`~/.cargo/bin/retoc`, checkout 885a8da; `retoc to-legacy --version UE4_26 --no-shaders --no-script-objects -f <substring>` converts in about a second per filter, no mappings file or key) plus hand-written readers (`probe/utoc_index.py`, 5-byte offsets are big-endian; `probe/uasset.py` tagged properties; `probe/rq_*.py` tables, curves and pieces; `measure_roboquest.py`, `measure_skeletal.py`). All 60 data tables parse, including enemy stats and the attack timings the wiki lacks; levels are generated from 151 authored pieces in 13 kinds, a fixed cell of about 130 to 160 m with four closable connectors; blueprint graphs were not decoded. Andrew asked whether enemy meshes could be ripped and resculpted: no (a derivative stays a derivative, forge provenance), so the pack carries an enemy design grammar plus a CC0 base-mesh shortlist instead. **Andrew keeps the Roboquest clone** as a private modding archive at `~/Projects/roboquest-mod/` (README, retoc, probe scripts; a parked modding ladder), recorded as the exception in ASSETS.md line 23 and TRACKING CS-03-A1; nothing in Scrapkeep reads it. Review lesson, third time: writers reproduce enum value sets; a mechanical gate (`probe/enum_gate.py`, per block, stems, both tally forms, planted control) is a review aid, not a pass gate, and large tables give it false positives. Brief writers up front: counts by unnamed tier, mode or type; public wiki vocabulary is fine.
