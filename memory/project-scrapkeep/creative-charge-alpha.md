On 2026-10-01, during the alpha program and right after his entry 10 play feedback, Andrew said: "I want you to be proactive and feel free to take creative liberty and charge with this as well. And make decisions and fill in the gaps or cut off the excess fat or like asynchronous features or things like that ... take charge and let's try to envision and craft this together."

**Why:** his feedback that evening was that mechanics "just happen" with no visible why (the Warden fight, synergies, map mechanics, enemies as blobs). He wants a coherent game more than every feature kept, and he does not want to be the bottleneck for design calls.

**How to apply (the interpretation is mine):**
- Make design and scope calls myself in the alpha program: pick defaults, fill unspecified gaps, and cut or merge features that cannot be made legible or that do not connect to the core loop. "Asynchronous features" I read as features out of step with the rest of the game.
- Log each call in `docs/decisions.tsv` and tell him what was cut and why in a short list he can veto. Do not send him menus of options for calls I can make.
- It does not replace the gates he set: his play decides feel and fun ([[fun-gate-before-alpha]]), heavy asset builds still show him a sketch first ([[natural-progression-storyboard-gate]]), and things he said he likes stay (the harpoon's homing smash, the v2 loop shape, fuller enemy mechanics, momentum per [[scrapkeep-design-principles]]).
- "Craft this together" means keep him in the vision: show direction early and cheaply, fold his mid-flight ideas in per [[mid-flight-direction-harmonize]].
- Still ask through AskUserQuestion for real preference forks ([[questions-via-askuserquestion]]), external or irreversible actions, and anything outside the plan's authorization.
