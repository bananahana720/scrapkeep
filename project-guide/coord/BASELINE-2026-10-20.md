## Method

- Ancestry and conflicts come from `git merge-base --is-ancestor` and `git merge-tree --write-tree` for each PR head against the stage head.
- Verdicts are the latest PASS or ACCEPT word in the PR's last 8 comments and reviews. The only CI check on these heads is GitGuardian, which is green on all of them, so gate receipts live in comments.
- `sketch:fast` ran on the stacked head first, then the full gate ran once on the final head. A red on the final head is bisected across the merges.
