# New project: name and goal

Paste these into the new project's name and description fields.

**Name:** Scrapkeep

**Goal (one line):** Ship a finished-feeling Linux alpha of Scrapkeep by wave 21. It opens from Andrew's app menu to a normal main menu and plays from the title through all three worlds and six dressed parks. It is accepted at his 11-area playtest sitting: no area marked "Not yet", no open crash or blocker bug, frame times in budget on his RTX 4080, and zero placeholders.

**What Scrapkeep is (for the description):** a Godot 4.7.2 roguelite. You pilot a rolling scrap-mech ball with a harpoon: bowl through enemies, grapple and slam, carve half-pipes, and grow mightier over a run, Binding of Isaac style, while the chaos scales with you. It has three worlds (Spillway, the Dig, the Highdraft) and three bosses (Brindle, the Waking, the Mind). The presentation is a melodramatic anime and comic playground with Scott Pilgrim as the touchstone; written lore stays in a dry crew-log voice. The music is jazz-rooted steampunk-cyberpunk with house and electro bass.

**After the alpha (parked until the sitting):** story and lore, characters, boss and system tweaks, then a roadmap toward a beta or a low-cost public release.
