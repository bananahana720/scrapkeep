Andrew settled these on 16 Oct 2026 in the "Alpha playtest playbook" thread. "Mine" means he took Claude's recommendation; "his" means he chose a different option.

## Goal
- Adopted the rewritten goal (mine): feature-complete exported Linux alpha, judged against the three pillars with outside evidence; accepted when no area is "Not yet", no open crash or blocker bug, and five first-time players reach the end of world one unaided. Doc: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/e68d8e33-f04a-44d5-8f00-a35bcd50cf95
- MVP framing (his): the build should feel finished. After the sitting, the remaining alpha work is story and lore, characters, bosses, areas and system tweaks.

## The sitting
- Build: the final full alpha after every remaining wave and fix (his).
- Who plays first: Andrew, from an outside tester's point of view (his).
- Verdict areas: 11 (mine). Core feel, Wardens and bosses, in-run growth, progression across runs, pilots, stage and world design, look, sound and music, clarity, difficulty and pacing, stability.
- Verdict choices: Accept / Accept with fixes / Not yet (mine).
- Results: logged as playtest entry 13; each Fail and fix note becomes a card for the wave coordinator (mine).

## Scope added to the remaining waves
- Fully dress all six park stages, including landforms and the six hero landmarks (KD7) (mine).
- Build the camp. This reverses the 6 Oct cut W18-R1 (his).
- Cosmetics: pilot paint stays in, and its runtime must be verified or finished (implied by the camp choice).
- Launch: an AppImage plus an app-menu entry, opening to a main menu with Play (to pilot select), Camp, Settings, Help and Achievements (his "Both"; the recommendation).
- Gamepad: optional; keyboard and mouse decide the alpha (his).
- Already in flight in wave 15: PARK-WIN (the stage 5 anchor and the stage 6 wall) and the stage-swap hitch.
