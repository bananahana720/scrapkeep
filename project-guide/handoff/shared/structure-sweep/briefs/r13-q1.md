## The unit

The player's reduced-motion setting is one yes-or-no fact, and today it is held in about fifteen places. This unit gives it one owner and deletes the copies. What a player sees with the setting on, and with it off, must not change, and neither may the settings file.

**What is there on your base.** `set_motion_statics(on)` in `sketches/core-loop/src/game.gd` writes the setting into: `EnemyMotion.motion_scale` (as 0.0 or 1.0), `HighdraftFx.preferred`, `WorldFx.preferred` (a property that forwards to `reduced_motion` in `src/presentation/world_fx/world_fx_kit.gd`), and a `static var reduced_motion` on each of `AbilityBar`, `UiTokens`, `AimReticle`, `RewardView`, `MossBubble`, `src/ui/caller_tile.gd`, `ClassCard`, `PilotAntics`, `ClassPreview`, `BossPlate` and `src/presentation/flourish_player.gd`; it also calls `set_water_calm(on)` on `src/presentation/spillway_scenery.gd`. `set_reduced_motion(on)` just below it then pushes the same value into live objects: the world effects' `set_quality`, the juice object, `player_fx`, the camera, and every `HighdraftFx` node. Readers pick whichever copy is nearest. Game-rule code reads the UI's copy: `UiTokens.reduced_motion` is read in `src/boss/requisition.gd`, `src/combat/miniboss.gd`, `src/combat/pit_jaw.gd`, `src/combat/warden_reactor.gd`, `src/world/cracked_cache.gd`, two files under `src/experimental/` and five under `src/presentation/`; and about thirty files ask the question as `EnemyMotion.motion_scale <= 0.0`.
