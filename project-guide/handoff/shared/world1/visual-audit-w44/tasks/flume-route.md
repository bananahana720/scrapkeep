**Flume level designer · world 1 map points 1, 2, 3 and 7.** Give a rider entering the siphon one readable decision: commit speed to the upper flume, or keep rolling down the lower return toward the same drum. [Top-down plate](../images/flume-plan.png) · [Replay and handoff](../HANDOFF.md)

2. On the kicker approach, take a rider-height still before the last turn and one on a missed landing. Try orienting an existing support or opening one gap in the dressing so the high lip frames the penstock tower, with the lower receiving turn visible below it. Use the worn outside curve of the lower line to show momentum through recovery. Keep hostile X marks and attack-ring edges reserved for their live events.
3. Ride the upper take, an intentional lower take and a missed upper take through the rejoin. In another pass, retain enemies and test the actual flume wave. Record when the landing and tower come into view, speed regained after the miss, and any floor warning hidden by a pier. Include a quiet stretch and the following encounter to check contrast in pace. A static clear cell is insufficient proof of downtime.
4. Run `park_landmark_sight_test`, `park_feature_census_test`, `park_breath_test`, `park_rhythm_test` and `park_cover_tags_test`. Add `lobber_cover_test` if a solid provides advertised cover. **Return** an overhead trace of the three takes and a three-shot strip of each approach, landing and rejoin.
