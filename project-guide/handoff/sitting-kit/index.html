Sitting Kit

Play the whole alpha once, as a stranger would. Mark each of the 11 areas Accept, Accept with fixes or Not yet. Every fix note becomes a card.

How to sit, and the keys
  1. Open the game from the app menu. Start a fresh profile so the first-time hints show.
  2. Play title to the end through all three worlds. Do the "Try" lines under each area as you go, and tick them off.
  3. Mark a verdict per area. Add a note for any fix: say what you saw and where.
  4. Under Coordinator calls, press Veto on any call you want undone. Keep is the default.
  5. Verdicts save as you click. Nothing is final until you press Export at the bottom.
