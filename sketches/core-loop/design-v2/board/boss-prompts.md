# Boss concept board: prompts

Written by `tools/concept/render_bosses.py --prompts-md`. Every frame uses Qwen-Image 2.1, 1344x768, 30 steps and the seed shown; a retry adds 100 per attempt to the seed. Every prompt ends with the shared style block below (the primer board's). A retry of a frame that has a revision inserts the revision and the retry style line before that block. Concept references only, never runtime assets and never admission evidence.

## Shared style block

Stylized chunky low-poly 3D game concept art with tactile hand-built surfaces. A greasy, workshop-built steam-cyberpunk American Southwest: dusty sandstone, worn paint, oily dark metal, mismatched bolted panels, weld beads, hose clamps and rubber. One coherent sunlit lighting condition with long canyon shadows and atmospheric distance. Readable silhouettes, clean composition. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no user interface, no watermark.

## r1: Reactor Warden: hero shot

Seed 3101.

Low third-person chase view from just behind a small sleek spherical mech, a clear glass sphere with a small fox pilot seated visible inside (a fox with pointed ears, a russet muzzle and a work cap, not a human), wrapped by two thin metal gyroscope rings that orbit it at angles, no legs, no arms, no wheels, with a faint mint green glow, rolling across cracked, blackened ground toward a slumped, hunched reactor hulk, an ancient cast containment vessel fused onto a tall beacon mast by its own meltdown. Its lower half is a wide ancient clamp: four heavy cast jaw plates biting around the base of the mast, the plates sized to close on a rolling sphere, half buried in sand. Above the jaws hunch two massive shoulders: one clean cast geometry, the other melted into frozen drips of dark slag. On its back three thick vent stacks swell and glow at their crowns. Its head is a cracked containment dome tilted down toward the player, split by one jagged crack that leaks intense electric blue light like a single eye. Dull dark petrol grey-green matte enamel, dusty and cracked, with thin glowing electric blue seams set in dark grooves; cast and seamless, no bolts, no welds, no rivets. Heat shimmer rises over the vents. On the ground before it a huge circular danger zone of bold diagonal red-orange and black hazard stripes fills toward the machine. Menacing, wounded, colossal presence. Golden hour, long shadows.

Retries add:

The machine's four jaw plates grip the base of one tall slender mast with a glowing ring on top. The dome's crack is the only eye. The player's sphere is clean glass with a fox inside and orbiting rings, tiny beside the machine. Stylized and simplified chunky shapes, painterly, not photorealistic.
