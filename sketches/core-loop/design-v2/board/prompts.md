# Primer visual board: prompts

Written by `tools/concept/render_board.py --prompts-md`. Every frame uses Qwen-Image 2.1, 1344x768, 30 steps and the seed shown; a retry adds 100 per attempt to the seed. Every prompt ends with the shared style block below. A retry of a frame that has a revision inserts the revision and the retry style line before that block. Concept references only, never runtime assets and never admission evidence.

## Shared style block

Stylized chunky low-poly 3D game concept art with tactile hand-built surfaces. A greasy, workshop-built steam-cyberpunk American Southwest: dusty sandstone, worn paint, oily dark metal, mismatched bolted panels, weld beads, hose clamps and rubber. One coherent sunlit lighting condition with long canyon shadows and atmospheric distance. Readable silhouettes, clean composition. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no user interface, no watermark.

## f01: Title

Seed 1001.

Wide cinematic title key art. Three small rolling salvage mechs, each a small irregular rolling ball of welded salvage the size of a car, lopsided with bolted scrap plates, with a clear glass cockpit dome on top where the animal pilot sits visible, rest on a sandstone mesa edge at golden hour, looking out over an enormous river gorge. The three balls look clearly different: one ringed by a mint-glowing plate collar with a rope spool, one with raked copper fins, one caged in an open truss with amber-and-black striped satchels. Round, lumpy balls with no wheels. Stylized and simplified chunky shapes, painterly, not photorealistic.
