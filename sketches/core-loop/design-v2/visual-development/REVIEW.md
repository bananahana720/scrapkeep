## Shared defects, excluded from selection

- The left bank suggests a bypass, but continuation beyond the Warden is hidden. A playable target must show and verify the entire escape route.
- The circular danger area lacks the charcoal boundary and diagonal pattern in the threat grammar. The radial segments are not an implementation specification. Runtime markings must consume the existing committed attack geometry and threat type.
- The horizontal ring still partly reads as armor. An alternate view and animation must establish independent gyro movement, clearance and the upright pilot.
- The Warden's piston competes with its blue opening. The selected working mechanism must determine its focal hierarchy.
- No still demonstrates rolling cockpit visibility, growth clearance, glass sorting, late-run effects, motion comfort or performance.

## Recommendation

A best balances tactile workshop materials, stylization and depth. B gives the clearest graphic separation but changes the look more strongly. C is a viable geometric direction, though its hero remains closer to A than its terrain does. Andrew selected A. Shared scene defects remain open.

The review did not approve the exact boss design, introduce a region, change collision, settle roster proposals or establish performance. A is the selected rendering treatment. B and C remain historical alternatives.
