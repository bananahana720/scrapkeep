Scrapkeep concept atlas in selected STYLE A, supplied image ONLY rendering/material reference. Tactile painted miniature with broad bevels, purposeful sparse wear, matte rubber, rust-red Fleet enamel and brass requisition tags. Seven distinct functional anti-ball designs on neutral gray studio floor, 3 panels top, 4 bottom, each with small action inset. Labels exactly PLATED BRUTE, DEFLECTOR, WINCH DRONE, GYRO JAMMER, SWATTER, RAMP FLIPPER, CATCH TRAP. Friendly scale cue is a clear spherical hull with visible seated fox pilot and separate copper gyro, no wheels. Large readable forms, no microtexture, no ornamental glow. Danger uses red-orange plus charcoal diagonal marks; harmless useful arrows bone-white. No ancient blue.
PLATED BRUTE: broad slow load-bearing machine carrying ONE thick replaceable impact plate bolted over vulnerable chest; action inset the extra plate has been broken off and lies on ground, body remains. Ground slam mechanism readable.
DEFLECTOR: huge concave dish shield on squat sprung tripod, dish defines silhouette; action inset ball rebounds up off shield lip with clean bone arc, launching rather than penetrating shield.
WINCH DRONE: high suspended four-rotor utility frame with hanging cable reel; action inset taut line pulls a ball toward two ground allies; drone is out of ram reach, exposed cable cut point visible.
GYRO JAMMER: paired eccentric coils spinning out of phase on wheeled industrial cart, directionally different from a magnet trap. Action inset ball's inner pilot stays seated while external gyro skews sideways; broad low striped field edge can be escaped.
SWATTER: low heavy paddle on pivot mast, wide sideways sweep, thick rubber paddle at ball midheight; action inset ball jumps above the paddle, clean vertical clearance.
RAMP FLIPPER: flush rectangular hinged floor plate with exposed hydraulic hinge; show horizontal ready state and tilted ramp state in same panel, ball leaves tangent in air; nonhazard back edge is visually distinct.
CATCH TRAP: open shallow hemisphere split into three padded curved jaws, ball fits its hollow; action inset closed jaws stop ball, second tiny pose breaking jaws outward. Shape must read catch cup, never spike trap or ramp.
Prioritize topology, lever arms, counterplay and distinct silhouette. Each has one designed function with believable source of force. No giant humanoid robot repeated seven times.
