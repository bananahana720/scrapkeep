Scrapkeep selected Style A painted workshop miniature concept sheet. Supplied reference gives material and lighting treatment only. Six large panels on warm gray: upper row three lesser ancient machine families, lower row three much larger stage Wardens. Ancient construction is smooth cast dull petrol enamel, deep charcoal recessed grooves containing restrained Cherenkov blue #3d8bff, old human manufacture repaired by animals. Distinct from Fleet's bolted rust-red. Broad clean forms and purposeful chips, no tiny photographic scratches, no black comic outlines, no sci-fi neon soup. One small clear sphere enclosing fox pilot shows scale beside each large Warden. Hazard red-orange with charcoal diagonal fill appears ONLY on floor in a warning inset, NEVER as permanent decorative armor. Blue seams identify machine lineage, do not indicate safe ground or damage zone.
Labels exactly REACTOR RELIC, SWARM REMNANT, MIND'S HAND, REACTOR WARDEN, HIVE WARDEN, MIND WARDEN. Each panel one main 3/4 design plus small activated/vulnerable state inset.
Reactor relic: squat cracked pressure vessel on stout industrial supports, circular bolted service collar, two articulated downward vents, inset vents open toward small fresh crater. Powerful contained heat, not fire elemental.
Swarm remnant: six small identical cast rotor pods fitting into an angular flying formation; inset a clear pilot ball bowls through and scatters pods. Readable same unit repeated, not six different creatures.
Mind's hand: elegant articulated four-finger manipulator on cast swivel base, sensing slit and load-bearing knuckles; inset hand braces a wall-facing plate against a repeated rebound, an exposed hinge reads alternative route. No magical eye or humanoid head.
Bottom Wardens share a recognizable heavy beacon clamp base: two massive curved shoes gripping an old circular beacon, with a broad unblocked banked bypass visible beside it. Different family-specific central machinery dominates silhouette. Reactor Warden has a stacked pressure reactor, shutters open to a single luminous core after release. Hive Warden is a broad cellular launch rack with empty sockets and unfolding drones, one central coupling exposed after launch. Mind Warden has three articulated cast arms adjusting braces around a quiet central core, one joint opens after it commits its counter. Show a warning pose and exposed mechanism inset for EACH, not merely recolors. Nothing guarantees exact boss geometry; these are candidate visual embodiments of established families.
