Scrapkeep STYLE A interface and transition study in same tactile painted-workshop world. 6 screens in2x3, charcoal shallow plates, worn bone text, restrained copper/brass hardware, clean icons and clear keyboard focus. Broad tactile workshop background, NO extra invented game modes or statistics. Labels and text only as specified, all legible.
PAUSE: dim but recognizable paused chase view behind compact panel, RESUME, SETTINGS, HELP, RESTART, RETURN; selected RESUME has bone outline and mint small focus pip. OPTIONS: simple sliders MASTER MUSIC EFFECTS, toggles REDUCED MOTION and SCREEN SHAKE, BACK; different focused/disabled states obvious by shape and value, no neon border everywhere. HELP: four large mechanism drawings: ball roll, jump, rebound off bank, hold gauge then release. Existing key bindings are unspecified here so neutral keycap glyphs only, no fabricated bindings. Short caption MOVE / JUMP / REBOUND / HOLD + RELEASE, BACK.
DEFEAT: sphere still contains living pilot in recoverable wreck, calm darkened scene, TRY AGAIN and RETURN, one small last-threat icon, no pile of unreadable stats and no dying animal gore. DEPARTURE: ball launches out of workshop to daylight along continuous chute, a narrow UI strip fades away while world route stays visible. RETURN TO PLAY: same scene settled into chase camera, aim reticle and compact ability slots restored, no obscuring wipe or jump cut. Small inset REDUCED MOTION uses direct dissolve rather than swoop, no camera shake. Focus returns to meaningful action. No long narrative copy, no full-screen white flash, no confetti. This is visual interface intent, existing exact controls and timings remain authoritative.
